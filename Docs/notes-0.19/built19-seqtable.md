## 33. Who does what: drag a message, the others make room
- Before: press and drag a message (iPhone/iPad: after a 0.25 s hold; Mac: at once) up or down. The message turned faint where it was, a copy followed the finger, a thin blue line showed the landing place, and on letting go everything jumped to the new order. Blocks followed only by their first/last message ids.
- Now: same gesture and same hold. The thin line is gone. As the finger passes other messages they glide up or down with a soft spring (about 1/3 s, small overshoot) to make room; the dragged message's own faint place sits in the gap where it will land; the copy under the finger is drawn from where it came from. Blocks stretch/shrink with the springs while dragging. Reduce Motion on = no gliding, everything goes straight to its place. Frames are drawn every frame only while dragging (`TimelineView`, paused otherwise).
- Blocks rule (Core, `moveStep`): a block keeps a message that lands between the others it held; a message dragged outside them leaves the block, which shrinks to what is left (first/last updated). A block that held only that message follows it. A message dragged into the middle of a block becomes held (as before). Before: moveStep did not touch blocks, so a first/last message moved out stretched the frame over everything between.
- Core: `Board.sequenceRoom(moving:to:before:)`, `SequenceRoom`, `SequenceLayout.shifted(...)`, `keepBlocksAfterMove` (Sequence19.swift). One undo step (`session.change { moveStep }`, unchanged). Read-only boards cannot lift (as before). Move up/Move down buttons and Option+arrows use the same `moveStep`, so they keep blocks right too.
- Not seen: how the spring feels on a real device; no auto-scroll while dragging near the screen edge (never existed).

## 34. Note box beside a message
- A chosen message's bar has a new chip "Note…" (id `sel.note`, SF Symbol `note.text`, sequence colour; lit when the message has a note). It opens a page "Note" with one field (placeholder "Words beside the message", id `note.field`, 1 to 5 lines), footer "A grey box beside the message. Leave it empty and press Save to take the note away.", buttons Cancel (`note.cancel`, Escape on the Mac) and Save (`note.save`, Return). Undoable (`session.change`). Only messages (not names, bands, sections).
- Drawn: small grey box (the app's "grey" soft plate with a grey line), top right corner folded (small triangle), caption size 11, to the right of the message (right of its lines, words and to-do ring, 12 pt gap), top aligned with the message's words. Width at most 150 pt, at least 54; text wraps. The message's row grows if the box is taller, and the diagram widens to hold the box. Pressing the box chooses its message. Core: `Board.setNote`, `SequenceNote` (size is a rough estimate: 6 pt per letter, 14 pt per line, 7 pt padding; the words are wrapped by the painter, so a very wide-lettered note can sit a little tight). Core `Item.note` only; copy/duplicate carry it.
- How it differs from the existing "Note" (kind `band`, tool "Note", `tool.note`): the band is a step of its own across all the lines, has its own place in the order, no number, and is written into the PARAGON text; the new note belongs to one message, has no place in the order, goes with that message when it moves, and stands beside it. Not written into the PARAGON text or "copy as text" (left out: not asked). It is drawn in the board's picture/PDF (same painter).

## 35. Table column widths
- A chosen table shows a small blue handle at the right edge of every column in the heading row. Drag a column's right edge (hit area 6 pt on the Mac/pointer, 14 pt for a finger; counted on screen) to make it wider or narrower; the table follows live. Least 40 pt, most 800 pt. Double-tap / double-click the edge: fit the column to its longest words on one line (also 40 to 800; the tick column goes back to its narrow self). Undoable: one step per drag or fit (`liveStart/liveEnd`, `change`). Not on read-only boards.
- Before: columns were only as wide as their words (56 to 220, longer words wrap). Now: a column with no hand width is still like that; a pulled column has `Item.widths[column]` (0 or missing = by words). Widths follow Add column / Remove column.
- The edge only reacts while the table is chosen alone (the handles show only then). A corner/size handle of the table keeps priority.
- Core: `TableRules.settingWidth`, `ownWidth`, `leastWidth` 40; `Item.tableSetWidth`, `tableClearWidth`.

## 36. Tick column
- Chosen table bar: new button "Tick Column" (id `sel.table.tick`, menu `menu.table.tick`, SF Symbol `checklist`, shown with its word on Mac/iPad, mark only on iPhone; lit when on). Pressing it adds a first column of tick boxes (not ticked) or, pressed again, takes that column away (its ticks go; Undo brings it back). A table of one column only loses the box mode and keeps the column. Not at the 20 column limit.
- Boxes: rounded square 16 pt, hollow (faint line) when not ticked; filled green with a white tick when ticked, the same green as the to-do done ring (`#2E8B57`, night `#3FA26C`). A tap on a box ticks / unticks (cell "x" / ""), also without choosing the table first; on ticking, the soft tick sound (`TickSound`) plays as for to-dos. The heading row of that column is an ordinary (empty) cell. Two presses on a box do not type; Tab in a table skips the boxes.
- Under the table, left: "3 of 8 done" (12 pt; faint, green when all are ticked). Counts rows under the heading only. Nothing is shown with no rows under the heading. The line is part of the table's height (selection ring includes it).
- Core: `TableRules.isTicked`, `tickCount`, `tickLine`; `Item.tableTickColumnOn/Off`, `tableToggleTick`. Columns added/removed before the tick column shift `tickColumn`.

## 37. Table sort by heading
- One tap on a heading cell sorts the rows under it by that column: first A to Z, second Z to A, third A to Z again; other column resets to A to Z. Heading row stays on top. Numbers by value (also "3,5"), words ignoring case/accents ("item 2" before "item 10"), numbers before words, empty cells always last in both directions; equal cells keep their order. A small chevron (SF Symbols `chevron.up` = A to Z, `chevron.down` = Z to A, grey) stands at the right end of the sorted heading while the cells still stand that way; any other change of the table (typing, row added) hides it and the next tap starts at A to Z. Undoable (one step). The sort works on a table that is not chosen yet (the first press also chooses it).
- Typing: two presses on a heading still type in it (the first press sorts, the second types, as before; the sort is kept). A tap while a heading is typed in does not sort: if it lands on the cell that was being typed in, that cell goes on being typed in; elsewhere it just ends the typing. The typing field itself takes its own presses.
- Core: `TableRules.sorted`, `nextSort`; `Item.tableSort`. Which sort was last done is kept per device in memory (`TableSortMemory`), not stored.
