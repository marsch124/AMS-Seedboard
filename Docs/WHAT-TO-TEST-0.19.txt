Seedboard 0.19 (build 19) - what to try. Nothing of this was seen on a real screen yet, so please look closely and tell me what feels wrong.

IMPORTANT
- A board changed in 0.19 opens read-only in 0.18. Update all your devices.

LIST (iPhone, iPad; the Mac where it says)
1. Swipe a row right: blue Pin. Swipe left: slate Put away. Short swipe = press the button; long swipe = done at once.
2. Search a word: a small grey line under the name shows where it was found, the word in bold.
3. Recent card: Sort menu (Last changed, Date made, Name).
4. Give a board a due day within a week: a Due soon card shows at the top.
5. Rows show a thin green done bar; on a timeline only "late" is red.
6. Hold a row: Colour... (no purple, no red) and Send to PARAGON....
7. Copy some lines, pull the list down and let go: a new mind map.
8. Mac: rest the pointer on a row: Quick look.

BOARD
9. Two quick presses on empty board: a sticky note (iPhone and iPad too now).
10. The + tile: press = note; hold = Note, Idea, Shape, Text.
11. More, Read mode: the board freezes, eyeglasses pill with Done at the top.
12. More, Background colour...: Paper, Warm grey, Night blue. Look by day and by night.
13. Pick colours: the last three come first.
14. Choose 3 notes, press Stack. Make one note Small. Choose things, Put in a frame (Mac Shift Command F).
15. Mac: Hide Sidebar with Control Command S. iPad: drag the grip on the tool bar to the left or right edge.

MIND MAPS AND ARROWS
16. Branches are thick near the middle. More, Number the branches: 1, 2, 3 badges.
17. Menu of a main branch: Move to the Other Side. More, Map look: Lines, Curves, Boxes.
18. Menu of an idea: Grow ideas from this: chips; press one.
19. Choose an arrow: drag the diamond knob to bend it; Straighten. Menu says Dashed (maybe).

TIMELINES
20. Drag a bar between rows: the others glide.
21. Hold a bar: big dates. Note on a bar: tap to read. Who: initials. Repeat: copies under the bar.
22. Weeks and Months switch above the zoom. More, Group by colour.

WHO DOES WHAT AND TABLES
23. Drag a message: the others make room. Add a Note... beside a message.
24. Table: drag a column edge; Tick Column (tick, "3 of 8 done"); tap a heading to sort (chevron).

PENCIL AND PICTURES
25. Draw a rough circle, box or arrow and hold still at the end: it turns clean.
26. A picture: Round, Print Frame. Camera tile (iPhone, iPad): take a photo.

PARAGON AND SHARING
27. Choose a frame: Share Picture of This Frame.
28. Send page: the Preview under What PARAGON gets.
29. Paste a checklist from Notes (- [ ] and - [x]): ticked lines come as done ideas.

Settings, How it works: Signs in this app has the new marks. What's new: 0.19 at the top.
