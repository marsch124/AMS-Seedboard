# Seedboard — 50 new ideas for the look and what it can do (20 Nov 2026)

Written after 0.17. None repeats IDEAS-0.9, 0.11 or 0.14, nor anything he turned down (small board pictures in the list, widget, groups on the home page, mind map as a list, two windows, weekly review, hand-drawn marks on notes …). ⭐ = my 10 strongest.

## Home list (1–8)
1. ⭐ Swipe a board row: right = Pin, left = Put away (iPhone and iPad).
2. Search shows the line where the words were found, with the words in bold.
3. Sort the Recent card by last changed, made, or name (one small menu on the card).
4. A "Due soon" card at the top when a board's due day is within 7 days.
5. Timeline boards say "2 late" in red on their row — red only ever means late.
6. Hold a row → "Colour…" to pick the board's dot colour yourself.
7. Pull the list down to make a new board from what is on the clipboard.
8. Each row gets a thin done bar under the name (5 of 9 done fills a little more than half).

## Board and canvas (9–18)
9. ⭐ Double-tap empty board = a new sticky note right there (double-click on the Mac).
10. The colour row shows the last 3 colours you used first.
11. Duplicate with a drag: hold ⌥ on the Mac (or two fingers on iPad) while dragging makes a copy.
12. A "Stack" button: chosen notes fall into one neat column.
13. Arrows can be bent (a middle knob) and can have a dashed line for "maybe".
14. Sticky notes in two sizes: normal and small, for quick tags.
15. Grow a frame round chosen things in one press ("Put in a frame").
16. Hold the + on a board to choose what to add: note, idea, shape or text.
17. Read mode: one press freezes the board so nothing moves by accident while you read.
18. Board background colours: paper, warm grey, night blue — still calm.

## Mind maps (19–25)
19. ⭐ Press Return on an idea = a sister; Tab = a child (keyboard and iPad keyboard).
20. Branch lines get thicker near the centre, like a drawn tree.
21. "Number the branches" — 1, 2, 3 in front of the main branches for an order.
22. Move a whole branch to the other side with one menu line.
23. A count beside a folded branch: "+6".
24. Mind map look choices: lines, curves, or boxes in a tree.
25. "Grow ideas from this" — one line suggests 3 related words to start from (on-device, nothing sent away).

## Timelines (26–32)
26. ⭐ Drag a bar between rows to change its order, with a soft gap opening.
27. A milestone can carry a short note that opens when you tap it.
28. Bars show their dates while you hold them, big enough to read.
29. A "Week view / Month view" switch at the top, besides zooming.
30. Group bars by colour: one press lines up all pink bars together.
31. Repeat a bar ("every Monday for 6 weeks") as a row of short bars.
32. A bar with a person's initials in a small round (who does it).

## Who does what and tables (33–37)
33. Drag a message up or down to change the order.
34. A "note" box beside a message (grey, like real sequence diagrams).
35. Table: drag a column wider; double-tap the edge to fit the words.
36. Table: a tick column (✓ boxes that count "3 of 8 done" at the bottom).
37. Table: sort by a column with one tap on its heading.

## Pencil and pictures (38–42)
38. ⭐ Shapes by hand: draw a rough circle, box or arrow and it becomes a clean one.
39. Pencil colours: the last 3 used stand in the tool bar.
40. A picture can be cropped round (a circle) for faces and plants.
41. Take a photo straight into the board (camera button on iPhone and iPad).
42. A picture frame: thin white border and a soft shadow, like a print.

## PARAGON and sharing (43–47)
43. ⭐ "Share a picture of this frame" — only the chosen frame, not the whole board.
44. Send to PARAGON shows a little preview of the note it will write, before Send.
45. Send to PARAGON straight from the list: hold a row → Send.
46. Paste a checklist from Notes and its ticked lines arrive as done ideas.
47. A board as a PDF with one page per frame (frames become pages).

## Mac and iPad (48–50)
48. ⭐ Mac: the board list can be hidden with ⌘0 for a full-width board.
49. iPad: the tool bar can be dragged to any edge of the screen.
50. ⭐ Mac: a small "Quick look" when you hover a board row — the board's first lines.
