# AMS Seedboard — specification, version 0.2

Written from the code as it stands for 0.2, as of 2026-11-18, and from the nine builders' notes in `Docs/notes-0.2` (snap, look, timeline, maps, arrows, touch, shapes, bringback, mac). Where a note and the code disagree, this document says what the code does. Where the code does something other than the owner's feature list, it is marked **Note**. The last three parts to be built (Pencil drawing 3.15, the sequence diagram "Who does what" 3.16, and the bar beside the chosen thing with the menu, copy / cut / paste and Home 2.9) are written from their builders' notes in `Docs/notes-0.2` (pencil, sequence, bar).

## 1. What the app is

AMS Seedboard is a thinking tool for iPhone, iPad and Mac (one target, iOS 17 and macOS 14 and later, bundle id `com.schabbauer.AMSSeedboard`, team `D24ENP83QQ`, URL scheme `seedboard`, App Store category Productivity). Its purpose: start a thought on an endless board (mind map, flow with shapes and arrows, sticky notes) or on a timeline board (bars on days), grow it, and hand it to PARAGON (the owner's notes app) as a note, a project or Inbox tasks.

Layout of the repository:

- `Core/` — Swift package `SeedboardCore`: every rule, no screen. Files of 0.1: `Board.swift` (data), `BoardEdit.swift` (add, link, move, copy, remove, hit-testing), `MindMap.swift` (tree rules, sides and layout), `Room.swift` (a growing map pushes things aside), `Shapes.swift` (a shape's size from its words, changing its kind), `Viewport.swift` (where the board stands on screen), `Typing.swift` (the line-break rule for ideas), `Outline.swift` (lists in and out), `Route.swift` (arrow routing, the 0.1 rule for sides), `Starters.swift` (the six starts), `Paragon.swift` (the hand-over text), `Safety.swift` (the storage decisions). New in 0.2: `Snap.swift`, `DotGrid.swift`, `Tidy.swift` (falling in line, the dots, Line up / Even gaps / Same size), `Home.swift` (search, "Changed …", colour in use), `Timeline.swift` (the timeline board), `Reorder.swift` (ideas in another order), `ProjectPlan.swift` (a project by the ticks, one branch alone), `Arrows.swift`, `ArrowEdit.swift` (where arrows run; ends, dashes, the next shape), `Fingers.swift` (press areas, the shove), `ShapeOutline.swift` (the four new shapes), `Lanes.swift`, `ParagonLanes.swift` (lanes in frames), `Kept.swift`, `KeptFiles.swift` (removed boards, earlier versions), `ExportAll.swift` (export and import), `Remember.swift` (opens where you left), `Outside.swift` (boards from outside, the device's search), `MenuRules.swift` (menu lines and letters). Tests in `Core/Tests/SeedboardCoreTests`. (**Note**: `Core/BoardTests.swift` and `Core/StartersTests.swift` beside `Package.swift` are stray copies that are never built.)
- `App/Sources/` — the screens. Of 0.1: `SeedboardApp`, `RootView`, `BoardView`, `CanvasView`, `CanvasDraw`, `BoardSession`, `AppModel`, `BoardStore`, `ParagonGate`, `SendSheet`, `SettingsView`, `Guide`, `Sample`, `BoardPicture`, `Theme`. New in 0.2: `HomeView`, `BoardThumb`, `Look` (home page, small pictures, calmer colours), `SnapShow` (guides and dots), `TimelineView`, `TimelineDraw`, `TimelineBars`, `TimelineSession`, `MapOrder`, `SendBranch`, `ArrowDraw`, `ArrowSession`, `TouchLayer`, `Press`, `FingerSizes`, `ShapePaths`, `ShapeDraw`, `ShapePlate`, `LanesView`, `KeptPage`, `BringBack`, `BringBackBars`, `SampleKept`, `Menus`, `Remember`, `Intents`, `Spotlight`, `NewMarks` (stand-in names for marks not yet in the icon library).

## 2. Screens and controls

All identifiers below are accessibility identifiers. Every identifier of 0.1 is kept.

### 2.1 Window, home page and the lines at the top

- One window, no New Window command. Mac default size 1100 x 760, minimum 640 x 480. Split view: boards list on the left (width min 200, ideal 226, max 320), open board on the right. On iPhone (compact width) the list comes first and a board opens over it; opening or closing a board switches the column.
- Nothing open (Mac and iPad): the home page `HomeView` (identifier `board.none`, kept, with its children contained). Background `Paint(scheme).canvas`, ignoring safe areas (no black beside a charcoal board at night). Content: padding 16 x 12, at most 1120 wide.
  - Header: sprout mark 30, the heading "Plant a seed" (`Txt.big`), "Every idea starts on a board." (`Txt.label`, secondary); at the right the full-colour green "New board" button `empty.newBoard` (kept), which opens `StarterPicker` as a pop-up (regular width) or a page from the bottom (compact width).
  - Cards lie in a grid of columns 150–210 wide, 10 apart. A card: plate `Paint.plate`, edge `Paint.edge` 1, corner 11, by day the same faint shadow as a shape; picture on top (1.6 : 1), a 1-point edge line, words below with padding 8 x 6.
  - `StarterCard` (one for each of the six starters, `Starter.allCases`): picture of the starter's own board (`Thumbs.board(of:)`), name (`.body` medium, 1 line), hint (`.caption`, secondary, 2 lines reserved). The blank starter shows a quiet green plus (26). Identifier on the home page `home.starter.<rawValue>`; in the chooser `starter.<rawValue>` (kept).
  - `BoardCard`: picture, 9-point colour dot + name (`.body` medium, 1 line), `board.changedWords()` (`.caption`, secondary; 3.14). An empty board's picture says "Empty". Identifier `home.board.<title>`.
  - Sections "PINNED", "RECENT" (not pinned, not sent), "SENT TO PARAGON" (purple): the same three groups and heading style as the list. With a search the cards are the found boards; nothing found → "No board has these words" (`home.noMatch`).
  - **Note**: there is no button that leads back to the home page once a board is open on the Mac or iPad; it shows at the start when no board is remembered, and whenever the open board goes away. Board cards have no press-and-hold menu (Rename, Pin, Colour and Remove stay in the list).
- Opens where you left (7.3): the board that was open comes back at its zoom and place.
- Settings: on the Mac its own window (520 x 460), opened from the app menu (Settings…), ⌘, or the `boards.settings` link at the bottom of the list (a `SettingsLink`); with `-settings` it is opened by a message to the app. On iPhone and iPad it is a page (sheet) with Done.
- Opening `seedboard://board/<UUID>` opens that board (rules in 2.2).
- The clash line (`BringBackBars.swift`, `AppModel.clash: ClashNotice?`; identifiers `clash.notice`, `clash.words`, `clash.button`, `clash.close`): a line across the top of the window, over list and board, on all three devices (`safeAreaInset`), in `Part.kept`. Text `KeptRules.clashLine(title)`: "Another device changed “<Title>”. The other version is kept under Earlier versions." Buttons: **Show** (opens the page of 2.7 at that board and closes the line) and a cross. One line at a time (the latest board). Raised by the store (`onClash`) when iCloud held a newer version than the one being saved (mine went to the copies); when a version from another device was set aside (`-away`); when iCloud had parked a version of its own (a conflict version or an "<id> 2.seedboard" copy) and the two differed; and by the app when another device's news lost against what was typed meanwhile.
- The removed-link line (`removedLink.notice`, `.words`, `.button`, `.close`): `AppModel.removedLink` is shown as "This board was removed on 12 Nov 2026." + **Bring back** + cross. Bring back = `AppModel.bringBack(removed:)`, then the board opens and "“<Title>” is back." is said. 0 days left (by the mark's date): "… Its 30 days are over: it cannot be brought back.", no button. No readable copy: "No copy of this board is left to bring back."

### 2.2 Boards list (`boards.list`)

- Top: `boards.new` (full-colour green button "New board") opens "Start with": the six starters as cards with drawings (`StarterPicker` in `HomeView.swift`; `starter.blank`, `starter.mindMap`, `starter.flow`, `starter.prosCons`, `starter.steps`, `starter.timeline`). On regular width a pop-up: 3 cards per row, each 150 (Mac) / 160 (iPad) wide, 8 apart; its size is worked out from the number of starters (six starters → 2 rows). On compact width (the iPhone) a page from the bottom: columns 150–240 (2 per row), detents 78 % of the screen and full height. Choosing one makes the board, saves it and opens it. The picker has no per-starter switch: a new `Starter` case appears by itself.
- Under it: the search field `SearchField` (`HomeView.swift`): search mark (`Glyph.new(.search)`, 16, green), plain field "Search boards" (`boards.search`), clear button (round cross, `boards.searchClear`, only when not empty); background primary 6 %, corner 8, padding 8 x 5. iPhone/iPad: no auto-capitals, no auto-correction, Search key. Mac: Escape empties it. The text lives in `RootView` (`@State search`), is given to the list and to the home page, and is not stored. Rule in 3.14.
- Groups, shown only when not empty, in this order: Pinned (`pinned == true`), Recent (not pinned, `sent == nil`), Sent to PARAGON (not pinned, `sent != nil`). A pinned board that was sent stays in Pinned. With a search the three groups show the found boards. The list is sorted by `changed`, newest first, when loaded, when iCloud brings news, and at every kept change (so the open board moves to the top as it is changed). A newly made or copied board goes to the top.
- Row `boards.row.<title>`: dot in the board's colour (9), title (one line), `countLine`. Chosen row (`Look.chosenRow`): a soft green tint (#2E8B57 at 14 % by day, #5FBF8A at 20 % by night, corner 8), the name `.body` bold in `Part.boards.text`, the second line `.secondary`, the dot in the board's own colour. A tiny dot (6 points, amber) at the row's end, `notInCloudDot` (spoken "Not in iCloud yet"), shows while the board's newest change is on this device but not in iCloud yet (6, rule 11); it is never shown when iCloud is off or with `-sampleData`. Context menu (right-click, or press and hold): Rename… (alert "Name of the board", Cancel, Save), Pin to the top / Unpin, Make a copy, Colour (Green, Pink, Orange, Blue, Teal, Purple), Open in PARAGON (only when `sent != nil`), Remove… (confirmation "Remove “<title>”?", button "Remove the board", message "It is kept for 30 days in the Removed folder.").
- iPhone row (compact width, `phone: true`, decided in `RootView` from the window's own size class): second line `"<changedWords> · <countLine>"`, and at the right `RowPicture` (60 wide, 1.6 : 1, corner 6, 1-point edge). Mac and iPad rows keep `countLine` only and no picture.
- Empty list text: "No boards yet. Press New board." Nothing found (and there are boards): "No board has these words" (`boards.noMatch`).
- Bottom: `boards.settings` (Settings) and `boards.version` ("<version> (<build>)").
- `countLine`: parts "N idea(s)", "N shape(s)", "N note(s)" joined with " · "; "Empty" when none. Shapes are the eight flow shapes (3.1). Text and frames are not counted. A timeline: "6 bars · 1 milestone · 2 phases" (each part only when not 0), or "Empty".

Rules of the list (AppModel):

- New board: `Starter.board()`, colour = `Palette.boards[count % 6]`. The title is the starter's name; if a title (case-insensitive) is taken it becomes "<name> 2", "<name> 3"… A blank board ("New board") is not numbered. (`AppModel.add(_:)` holds this; the Shortcuts actions use the same path, 7.1.)
- Make a copy: new id, title + " copy", then " copy 2", " copy 3"… while a title (case-insensitive) is taken, `created` and `changed` now, `pinned = false`, `sent = nil`, `sentTodos = []`; if the board is open, its live state is copied. It opens.
- Remove: the open session is dropped, the pending save cancelled, the board leaves the list, a removal mark is left and both copies move to Removed (see 6), flash "Board removed. It is kept for 30 days in the Removed folder." (**Note**: the flash line only shows inside an open board, so it is not seen when a board is removed from the list.)
- Rename: trimmed; empty is refused. A change to a board that is not open is written at once. A board from a newer format cannot be renamed, pinned or coloured (refused), and a copy of one is shown but never written (see 6).
- Saving: every change of the open board is shown in the list at once and written 0.5 s later (also when another board is opened, and on `saveNow`). Words are handed over as they are typed, not only when the typing ends. When the app is left (scene no longer active) or closed (Mac: will-terminate), `AppModel.leaving` finishes the typing (`commitEdit`), saves at once and waits until the DEVICE copy is written (`flush`, 2 s at most). iCloud's copy follows by itself: on iPhone and iPad the app asks iOS for background time (`beginBackgroundTask` "Seedboard saves", ended when iCloud's step is done or the time is up); on the Mac, when the app closes for good, it waits up to 2 s more for iCloud's copy.
- A board that another device removed leaves this list too; if it was open, its typing is finished first: when the board was changed after the removal it stays, is saved again and comes back on every device (6, rule 5); otherwise it closes. A board removed here is not put back by a late save.
- News that iCloud brings for the open board while the owner is typing or dragging in it is HELD (not shown, not thrown away, not marked as seen) and offered again the moment the typing ends: without a change of his own the other device's version is shown; with a change his is the newer one and stays, the other version goes to `SafetyCopies` at the save that follows, and the clash line of 2.1 says so. Opening another board also ends the typing and offers the held news.
- A link `seedboard://board/<id>` (5.3) that arrives before the boards are loaded (the app was started by it) is held and followed once loading has finished. A board that is not on the device: if it has a removal mark, the removed-link line of 2.1 shows (with Bring back); otherwise "That board is not on this device yet. It opens when it arrives." and the board opens by itself if iCloud brings it before another board is opened. A result of the device's search and the Open board action follow the same path (7).
- Colour name of a board is one of `Palette.boards`: green, pink, orange, blue, teal, purple.

Title = board title (inline on iOS).

More menu: Rename… (alert, text field `rename.field`), Pin to the top / Unpin, Paste a list as a mind map (clipboard text; flash "Nothing to paste." if none; greyed on a timeline), Copy the board as text (flash "The board's words are copied."), Share a picture… (share sheet, PNG named "<title>.png", the same picture as for PARAGON; not shown for an empty board), Remove the board… (same confirmation as the list).

Tool bar `board.tools` (a white plate, corner 15, a thin edge and a soft shadow; its own accessibility element with the buttons inside; scrolls sideways when too narrow): eight tools, each 40 x 40 (on iPhone and iPad with a press area of 46 x 46):

The plate with all shapes (`ShapePlate.swift`): `ShapePlate` has a heading in the shapes colour with Cancel (Escape), 8 tiles in 2 rows of 4 (the order of `ItemKind.flowShapes`: Box, Round box, Oval, Diamond, Person, Document, Slanted box, Store), each 54 high: the mark (26) with the kind's word under it; the current kind in full colour. Width 344 as a pop-up; the full width as a sheet. `PlatePresenter`: a pop-up when the width is regular (Mac, iPad, a big iPhone on its side), a sheet with a height detent (230; Lanes 250) when it is compact (iPhone). `ShapeTool`: the tile shows the mark of the shape picked last; the corner mark is a small filled corner (7 points; help "All shapes"); on the iPhone the corner only shows and takes no presses. The last pick is kept on the device (`UserDefaults` key `tool.lastShape`); Diamond is never kept (it has its own tile); with `-sampleData` nothing is read or kept (`ItemKind.boxTile(stored:)`). Identifiers: `plate.shapes`, `plate.shape.<stored name>` (adding), `plate.cancel`.

Selection bar (`board.selectionBar`, its own element with the buttons inside), shown above the tools while something is chosen and the board is not read-only. ### 2.4 Send page (`SendSheet`)

Title "Send to PARAGON". Rows `send.kind.note` ("As a note"), `send.kind.project` ("As a project"), `send.kind.todos` ("Only the to-do boxes") with explaining lines. The words of the rows: note — "The board's picture and its words as a tidy outline. It goes to Resources, and one line in the Inbox points to it." (timeline: "The timeline's picture and its bars with their dates. …"); project — "The branches become headings. The ideas you ticked To do become tasks; with nothing ticked, all of them do. It goes to Projects." (timeline: "The phases become headings, the bars become tasks with their start and due day. It goes to Projects."). Field `send.title` (name; note and project only; starts as `noteTitle(for: board.title)`). A line says how many to-do boxes become tasks; when "As a project" is chosen it is replaced by the project line `send.projectLine` (5.6). When the board was sent before: "Last sent <yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm> as a <kind>: “<title>”" and `send.openLast` ("Open in PARAGON"). Fixed line: "Seedboard only adds something new. It never changes a note you already have; sending again makes a second note." Errors in `send.problem` (red). Buttons `send.cancel` (Escape) and `send.send` (Return; label "Sending…" while busy; full-colour purple, paler when disabled).

The branch switch `send.branchOnly` (`SendBranch.swift`) shows only when exactly one idea is chosen; off at first: a row with the mark `.branch`, the words "Only the branch “<words>”" (a centre: "Only the map “…”"; no words: "Only this branch"; words longer than 32 letters are cut to 30 + "…") and a drawn switch 38 x 22 (PARAGON purple when on). Its accessibility value is "On" / "Off". When on, one line under it: "Just this idea and what hangs under it is sent, with a picture of it alone. The board is not marked as sent." Rules in 5.7.

Size: Mac 470 wide, as tall as its content. iPhone and iPad: the content (at most 560 wide) sits in a scroll view that only bounces when it does not fit; the only detent is large, so on the iPhone (and on an iPad in a narrow window) the page is full height and the title is never cut; on the iPad in a wide window the page is a card sized to its content (`presentationSizing(.form.fitted(horizontal: false, vertical: true))`, iOS 18 and later; on iOS 17 the system's own card size). When the page closes the board takes the keys back.

- If PARAGON is not found: `send.notReady` shows the line from Settings; Send is disabled.
- Send is enabled when PARAGON is found, not busy, and: for "Only the to-do boxes" at least one unsent to-do box with words AND an Inbox list reachable; for the others the board is not empty.
- On open the kind is "note", except when the board has no mind map, unsent to-do boxes exist and every item is a to-do box: then "to-do boxes".
- After success the page closes, the board is saved with `sent` and `sentTodos`, and a flash line says what happened.

### 3.2 File format

- `Board.currentFormat = 2` (0.1: 1). Raise it when a new version writes something an older version would lose or could not use: a new stored field, a new kind of item or arrow. The test `StoredFieldsTests` (in `SafetyTests.swift`) holds the list of stored field names for each format number and fails when a field is added, renamed or dropped without a new number.
- What format 2 adds. Board: `kind`. Item: `side`, `start`, `end`, `row`, `lanes`, `strokes` (3.15), `from`, `to`, `answer` (3.16); the kinds `bar`, `milestone`, `phase`, `person`, `document`, `slanted`, `store`, `drawing` (3.15), `who`, `message`, `band`, `section` (3.16). Board `kind` may also be "sequence". Arrow: `fromSide`, `toSide`, `tail`, `dashed`. The golden list for format 2 — board: changed, colour, created, format, id, items, kind, links, pinned, sent, sentTodos, title; item: bothSides, colour, end, folded, h, id, kind, lanes, order, parent, row, side, start, text, todo, w, x, y; arrow: colour, dashed, from, fromSide, head, id, style, tail, text, to, toSide; sent: kind, path, title, when. All the new fields are optional and are left out of the file when not set (`encodeIfPresent`).
- `lanes` on a frame: `{ "way": "rows" | "columns", "names": [text …], "cuts": [0…1 …] }` (3.12).
- Which number a file carries: a new board of any kind is made with format 2. A board read from a file keeps the number it had (a file without `format` reads as 1) until it is changed: the first change (`touch`) raises it to 2. So a 0.1 board that 0.2 only shows stays format 1; a board 0.2 changed is format 2, and Seedboard 0.1 shows it read-only (0.1's own rule for a newer format) until that device is updated. This app shows a format-3 board read-only in the same way. (**Note**: the timeline builder's note says "a board loaded with format 1 keeps 1"; that holds only until the board is changed.)
- Encoding: JSON, pretty-printed with sorted keys, dates as ISO 8601 written to the thousandth of a second (e.g. `2026-11-18T09:15:00.123Z`); reading accepts them with or without the fraction.
- Tolerant reading: a missing field gets its plain default (kind "box", text "", x/y 0, w/h the kind's default size, colour "", todo/folded/bothSides false, parent nil, order 0, the format-2 fields nil; board title "Board", created 1970, changed = created, format 1, pinned false, colour "green", sentTodos []). A field this version does not know (on the board, on an item, on an arrow; any JSON value) is CARRIED: it is kept as it was read (`extra`) and written back untouched at every save, so an older app never drops what a newer one wrote. (Fields inside `sent` are not carried.) An unknown kind word draws as a box and an unknown style as right angles, but the word is kept and written back as it was, and such a board is read-only (`canBeChanged` false: the banner of 2.3, no tools, never written over, no copy) exactly like a board of a newer format. One broken item or arrow is skipped without losing the board (each element is read by itself). An arrow without `from` or `to` is skipped. A board without an `id` cannot be read. A damaged file in which an idea hangs under itself, or ideas hang in a ring, opens: such an idea becomes a centre (each ring member its own map) and `descendants` never goes round for ever. Lanes whose cuts make no sense are read as even lanes; fewer than 2 names are filled up to 2.
- A file with `format` above 2, or one that holds a kind of item or arrow this version does not know, comes from a newer version: it is read as far as this version understands it and SHOWN, but `canBeChanged` is false (the board is read-only, 2.3) and it is never written over (see 6).
- Not in any board file: the place and zoom of the view (7.3), the last shape picked (2.3), the time of the last export (6.2), the scale of a timeline.

### 3.4 Mind maps (MindMap.swift, Reorder.swift)

- An idea with no parent is a centre; every other idea hangs under one parent. `children(of:)` are sorted by (`order`, id string). Depth 0 = centre, 1 = main branch.
- Layout constants: `gapAcross = 46` (sideways, between an idea and its children), `gapDown = 10` (between sisters). The centre stays where the owner put it. Every other idea is placed by `layoutMaps()` after every change: the height of a subtree is the larger of the idea's own height and the sum of its children's subtree heights plus gaps (a folded idea counts as its own height); children are stacked top to bottom in order and each is centred in its slot; a child's x is the parent's right edge + 46 (left side: parent's left edge − 46 − the child's width). With `bothSides`, every main branch is placed on the side it remembers (`side`, see Sides below); on each side the branches are stacked in their order. A main branch carries its side to everything below it. An idea whose parent is gone, or that hangs under itself, becomes a centre; ideas hanging in a ring each become a centre. Folded: children are not placed and count as hidden (also not hit, not drawn, not counted by `bounds`).
- Sides (`MindMap.sides(of:bothSides:)`, one rule for the layout, `side(of:)` and the drawing): without `bothSides` everything is right. With it, a main branch stays on the side it remembers. A branch that remembers none goes to the emptier side (right when even), in order. A map in which NO branch remembers a side (a file from 0.1, a pasted list, a starter, a one-sided map just switched to both sides) is split as in 0.1: the first `(n + 1) / 2` right, the rest left. `layoutMaps()` and `settleSides(of:)` write the sides down, so adding or removing a branch never makes another one jump across the middle. Switching to one side keeps the remembered sides for the way back.
- Adding: `addCentre` (150 x 46, both sides on); `addChild` appends after the last sister (`order` = last + 1), takes the next free colour of `Palette.branches` if it is a main branch (first not used by its sisters, else by count), unfolds the parent; a new main branch of a both-sides map goes to the emptier side (right when even). `addSister(above:)` puts the new idea right after (or before) this one and renumbers 1, 2, 3… (for a centre it makes a first branch); a new main branch made this way stays on the side of the idea it was made from when the two sides are even, else it goes to the emptier side.
- `reparent(id, under:)`: refused when the target is the idea itself or lies below it, or either is not an idea; the idea goes last under the target, the target is unfolded; under a centre an idea with no colour takes the next free branch colour and (unless it already was a main branch there) the emptier side; under a non-centre the colour and the side are cleared. The idea may be a centre: its whole map then hangs under the target, its `bothSides` goes off and every idea below it loses its own colour and side, so all of them follow the branch they are now in.
- `detach(id, at:)`: the idea becomes a centre at the point (colour and side cleared, both sides off). The ideas right under it are main branches now: each one without a colour takes the next free branch colour.
- `moveOut(id)` (Shift+Tab, "Out one level"): the idea becomes the sister right after the idea it hung under (`reparent` under the grandparent, then renumbering). Refused for a centre and for a main branch. An idea that becomes a main branch this way takes a branch colour and stays on its old parent's side.
- Another place among sisters (new in 0.2):
  - `Board.shiftIdea(_:down:) -> Bool`: swaps the idea with the next / previous sister *of its side* in the stored order (`order` is renumbered 1, 2, 3 …). Parent is a centre with `bothSides`: sides are written down first (`settleSides`); at the end of a side the branch's `side` flips and it is put last (down) or first (up) in the whole order, so it stands at the bottom / top of the other side. Elsewhere, at the end: nothing, returns false. A centre: false. `canShiftIdea(_:down:)`, `sistersOnSide(of:)`, `place(of:) -> (at, of)`, `slot(of:)`.
  - `IdeaSlot { parent, side, before }` — a place among the sisters of one side; `before == nil` = after the last. `IdeaDrop`: `.springBack`, `.hang(under:)`, `.reorder(IdeaSlot)`, `.tearOff`. Constants: `MindMap.tearOff = 120`, `MindMap.sisterEdge = 0.25`, `MindMap.shortestLine = 40`.
  - `Board.ideaDrop(_:at:reach:)` — the decision when a dragged idea is let go, in this order: (1) the point is over another idea (`idea(at:notIn:)`, not itself, not one below it): over its own parent → `.springBack`; over a sister only when the point is in the middle half of her height (between 25 % and 75 %) → `.hang`; over any other idea → `.hang`. (2) For the idea's own side, then (main branch of a both-sides map only) the other side: the box round all sisters of that side (the idea's own frame included on its own side), grown by `reach` on every side. Point inside → the slot is "before the first sister whose middle is below the point", else after the last. The slot it already has → `.springBack`, otherwise `.reorder(slot)`. (3) Otherwise `.tearOff`.
  - `Board.placeIdea(_:in:) -> Bool` — puts the idea before `slot.before` (or after the last sister of `slot.side`), renumbers `order`, and gives a main branch of a both-sides map `slot.side`.
  - `Board.landingLine(for:moving:)` — the line's two ends: y = middle of the gap between the two neighbours (first place: 5 above the first sister; last place: 5 below the last, half of `gapDown` 10); length = the idea's width, at least 40; it starts at the sisters' shared edge (left edge on the right side, right edge on the left side).
  - App: `reach` is `120 / scale` board points, i.e. always 120 points on screen (`BoardSession.tearOffReach`; in 0.1 an idea tore off after 50 screen points in any direction). `BoardSession.shiftIdea(_:down:)`: one `change` = one undo step; while that idea is being typed in, the move joins the typing's undo step and the typing goes on; then `reveal`. `BoardSession.dropHints(for:at:)` / `dropIdea(_:at:)` (`MapOrder.swift`): what is shown while dragging and what happens on letting go — `.reorder` → `change { placeIdea }` (one undo step), the idea stays chosen; `.hang` → `hang`; `.tearOff` → `detach` (notice "Made its own map" with Undo); `.springBack` → nothing.
  - The landing line (`CanvasView.landing`): `Paint.chosen` (green by day #2E8B57, by night #5FBF8A), 2.5 points wide on screen, round ends, a small dot (radius 3.5 on screen) at each end. Drawn only during an idea drag.
- `idea(at:notIn:)`: the topmost visible idea under a point (box grown by 5 across, 4 up/down), leaving a set of ideas out — used while a whole map is dragged over another.
- Making room (`makeRoom(since:)`, Room.swift), called by the app after every undoable change and every keystroke with the board as it was before: every map whose visible idea boxes changed while its centre did not move pushes away what it NEWLY covers — a note, shape or free text that was not moved by the change, overlaps an idea box now and overlapped none before, and likewise another map as a whole (centre not moved; all its ideas go together, hidden ones too). Each goes the shortest of the four ways (right, left, down, up; away from the centre first when two are equal) until it stands 8 points clear of every idea box of the map. A pushed note, shape or text takes along, the same way, any other note, shape or text it would then newly lie on (at most 60 such pushes). Frames and things the owner laid on a map on purpose stay. A dragged map (centre moved) pushes nothing. Undo, Redo, a live drag and a version from another device do not make room.
- Branch colour: a main branch's own colour (pink if empty); everything under it follows. Side: see Sides above.
- Walking (`idea(from:step:)`): up/down = the neighbouring sister on the same side (not past the ends); on a centre right/left = its first child on that side; on another idea the key pointing towards its children = its first child (none if folded), the other = its parent.
- Removing an idea removes everything under it.
- Text sizes: centre 17 bold, main branch 15 bold, deeper 15 regular. Fitting (`Lettering.fit`, after every change): the words are measured (wrap width centre/idea 240, free text 360); centre w = max(110, text + 32), h = max(44, text + 20); other idea w = max(36, text + 6), h = max(26, text + 8); free text w = max(40, text + 8), h = max(26, text + 6); shapes: see 3.5. Note max(own, text + 22) (wrap width − 20); an empty idea is measured as "Idea". Shapes and notes never shrink.
- Branch drawing: a curve from the parent's side middle to the child's near side middle, 2.6 wide, in the branch colour.
- A folded idea shows a filled dot (grey for a centre, else the branch colour) with the number of its direct children, 14 points beside it.

### 3.5 Shapes, arrows and routing (BoardEdit.swift, Shapes.swift, ShapeOutline.swift, Route.swift, Arrows.swift, ArrowEdit.swift; painters ShapePaths.swift, ShapeDraw.swift, ArrowDraw.swift)

**Shapes**

- `add(kind, centre:)`: a note with no colour becomes yellow; a frame is inserted at the bottom of the item list (so it is drawn under everything); everything else is appended (on top).
- Look: box = rounded rectangle radius 9; round = capsule; diamond = four tips; oval = ellipse. Uncoloured: white/plate fill with a 1.4 grey edge; coloured: soft tint fill and 1.8 coloured edge; round joins and ends. By day every one of the eight shapes has a faint shadow under its fill only (`.shadow(color: black 6 %, radius: 2, x: 0, y: 1)`); none by night. The mind-map centre, notes (own shadow), text and frames have none of it. Night check of the 9 palette colours (unchanged values): light ink on the tinted shape fill 9.3–11.6 : 1; outline/branch/arrow colour on the board 7.4–11.1 : 1; outline on its own fill 5.5–6.7 : 1.
- The four new outlines (`ShapeOutline`):
  - Person: the figure takes the top 62 % of the height (`figurePart`). Head: a circle, radius 24 % of the figure's height, 1 below the top. Shoulders: the upper half of an oval, starting 6 % of the figure's height under the head, standing on the figure's base line; as wide as 1.15 x the figure's height, at most the width − 4. Words: under the figure, 4 from each side, from 4 below the base to 2 above the bottom, set in the middle of that room. Height for words: (words + 6) / 0.38, rounded up.
  - Document: top corners rounded by 7. The bottom edge is a wave that swings `min(9, 14 % of the height)`: its middle line lies one swing above the bottom; the left half dips to the bottom, the right half rises one swing above the middle line. Words: 12 from each side, 9 from the top, down to 4 above the wave's highest point. Height = words + 32.
  - Slanted box: the top edge is pushed `min(18, 20 % of the width)` to the right of the bottom edge. Words: inside the upright part, 6 from each slanted side, 11 from top and bottom. Height = words + 22.
  - Store: an oval lid and an oval bottom, each `min(9, 14 % of the height)` high as a half; the lid's front lip is drawn as a line. Words: 10 from each side, 3 under the lip, 3 above the start of the bottom curve. Height = words + 34.
  - `ShapeOutline.textBox(kind, frame)` gives the room for words for all 8 kinds; `ShapeOutline.holds` says whether a point is inside the drawn outline. Typing in place: the field covers the text box (`Item.typingFrame`). To-do tick: top right corner as before; for a Person beside the head (3 inside the right end of the shoulders, 6 below the top) (`ShapeOutline.tickSpot`).
- Size from the words (`ShapeFit.size`): needed height = text + 22 for box and round (wrap width − 24), 2 x text + 20 for a diamond (wrap 0.56 of width), 1.4 x text + 16 for an oval (wrap 0.72 of width); the four new kinds as above (`ShapeFit.wordsWidth` / `ShapeFit.height`). Normally the width is the owner's and only the height grows: max(own, needed). While its words are typed, and right after its kind is changed, a shape whose words no longer fit its height first grows WIDER, round its middle, to the narrowest width at which they fit (found in 2-point steps), at most to `ShapeFit.widest` (box, round, document and store 300, oval 320, slanted 330, diamond 340, person 220), and only then taller. A shape the owner made wider than that is never narrowed.
- Changing the kind of a shape (`Board.setKind(_:to:)`) keeps the middle, the words, the colour and the arrows, and keeps the size when the owner (or the words) changed it; only a shape still exactly at its old kind's default size takes the new kind's default size. After the change the shape is fitted with widening. Any of the eight kinds can be changed into any other.
- Resizing (`resize`) never goes below 44 x 28 (frame 120 x 80, and with lanes never so small that the narrowest lane falls under 60: `Item.leastFrameSize`).
- Notes: paper colour, radius 4, soft shadow, left-aligned words, dark ink by day and by night. Frame: radius 14, soft tint (0.45 day / 0.35 night) with a 1.6 line, name at top left in 13 bold, default colour teal, placeholder "Frame".
- Letter keys are unchanged (B Box, U Round box, O Oval, D Diamond); the 4 new kinds have no key, and B always adds a Box whatever the tile shows.

**Joining and the next shape**

- `link(from:to:)`: refused for the same item, a missing item, or a frame at either end; the same (from, to) pair is never made twice (the old one is returned). Arrows may join shapes, notes, text and ideas.
- Word of a new arrow out of a diamond (`Board.wordForNewArrow(from:)`): no arrow leaves it yet → "Yes"; one leaves → "No" ("Yes" if that one says "No"); two or more → none. Applies to `addNext` and to `link(from:to:)` when no words are given (plus drag, arrow tool).
- `addNext(from:direction:)`: not for ideas or frames. `ItemKind.nextInFlow`: diamond → box, round → box, everything else itself. A changed kind gives a plain Box (150 x 56, no colour); the same kind copies size and colour. The new shape is placed middle on middle with its source, joined by an arrow. Gap: 70; 100 when the new arrow carries a word. While its box grown by 8 touches a visible non-frame item (up to 12 tries) it steps aside: down by its height + 24 for left/right steps, right by its width + 24 for up/down steps.
- Tab (no Shift, ⌥, ⌃) with exactly one flow shape chosen, or while its words are typed (`BoardSession.flowKey`): `addNext` in `Board.flowDirection(at:)`. ⌘ + arrow key: `addNext` in that direction. The words being typed are kept first. Mind-map ideas, notes, text and frames are not touched (Tab on an idea is unchanged). `flowDirection`: the opposite of the side at which the oldest arriving arrow arrives; none: right. A diamond that already has an arrow leaving on that side: down (when the flow runs sideways) or right (when it runs up or down), if that side is still free.
- The plus dragged out of a diamond or round box and dropped on empty board also makes a Box. (**Note**: a shape pulled out of a plus takes the kind's default size; one made by pressing the plus takes the size of the shape it comes from.)
- Removing items removes the arrows that lose an end, and forgets their `sentTodos`.

**Where an arrow runs** (`Board.arrows()` → `[id: Route.Arrow]`; `Board.arrow(id)`)

- Left out: arrows with a missing end, an end folded away, or both ends on the same item.
- Sides: the stored side (`fromSide`, `toSide`) if there is one, else the rule of 0.1 (`Route.sides(from:to:)`): "apart across" = the boxes do not overlap in x; "apart down" likewise in y. Sideways when apart across (and, if also apart down, when |dx| >= |dy| between centres), up/down when only apart down; when they overlap both ways, by |dx| >= |dy|. The line leaves the side facing the other item.
- Sharing a side (`Route.offsets`, `Route.room`, `Route.spread` = 14): the arrows on one side of an item (leaving and arriving alike) are put in the order of where their other end lies (by its middle, top to bottom on a left/right side, left to right on a top/bottom side; equal: older arrow first) and stand 14 apart round the middle: −7/+7, −14/0/+14 … The room is the side's length less 16 (60 % of it on a diamond or an oval); with less room they share what there is. If exactly one of them runs dead straight (facing sides whose middles line up within 1 point), it keeps the middle and the others stand 14 apart beside it. An arrow that is alone on a side of a box, note, text or round box slides along that side (at most half its room) to stay straight when its other end had to make room.
- Meeting the outline (`Route.anchor`): off the middle, the end lies on the real outline — the slanted edge of a diamond, the curve of an oval, the round end of a round box (corner radius 9 on a box). For the four new shapes the end is pulled in by `ShapeOutline.inset` of that side: Slanted box left/right = half the slant (9 at 160 wide); Document bottom = the swing (9); Person left/right = up to the shoulders at mid-height (24 at 110 x 96); 0 everywhere else.
- Right angles (`Route.elbow`): facing sides: one straight piece when the ends line up within 1 point, else one bend half way (as in 0.1); arrows side by side bend one after the other (the bend moves by the arrow's place on the side), so they never share a piece. Any other pair of sides (after a side was chosen): the line leaves each side straight out for 16 points (`Route.reach`) and takes the way with the fewest corners, then the shortest (a corner counts as 24 points): straight on, one corner, two corners in one channel, three corners in two channels. Channels: half way between the two items, and 16 outside them.
- Round a shape: when the plain line would run through a shape, sticky note or idea (its box grown by 2), channels 12 points off the edges of the six nearest such things are added (`Route.clearance` = 12) and the best way that touches nothing is taken (simple ways before long ones). Free text and frames are passed over. Things that lie on one of the two ends are not counted. No way found: the plain line stays.
- Straight: a straight line between the two ends; blocked: past one corner of the shape in its way, 12 off, when that keeps 6 clear of everything; else along one side of it (two corners); else past one corner however close; corners rounded 8.
- Curved: a curve that leaves each side straight out (pull = half the distance along that side's direction, at least 40 when the sides do not face each other). When the curve itself would run through something: the Straight way round, with wide corners (radius 24).
- Corners (`Route.rounded`): radius 8, or half the shorter leg beside the corner; under 0.5 the corner stays sharp.
- Heads (`Route.head`): 9 long, 5.5 to each side, tip on the item, pointing along the last piece of the line (so a Straight arrow's head follows its slant). Both ways: a second head at the start. No ends: none.
- Line: 2.2 wide, round caps and joins, grey (7B8794 / A9B4C4) or the arrow's colour; Dashed: 5 on, 6.5 off (looks like 7 and 4 with the round caps); heads are never dashed. Chosen: a soft 8-wide halo.
- Words (`Route.words`): 13 medium, ink colour (or the arrow's colour), no patch behind them (in 0.1 they sat at half the length of the line on a plate of the canvas colour). Beside the first piece of the line: a level piece — the corner of the words 12 along and 4 above the start; below instead when the arrow is the lower of several on its side, or stands alone and the line then climbs. An upright piece — 8 to the right and 10 along; to the left when the arrow is the left one of several. To the left of a start that runs left the words run leftwards. If the first piece is shorter than 48 and the line turns to the side the words stand on, they stand beside the first later piece of 48 or more. Straight and Curved: on the side the line moves away from (when it moves more than 20); a Straight arrow that shares its side stands its words 28 further along. (**Note**: the shapes builder's note says the words sit in the middle of the shortened line; the arrows builder's painter, which is the one in the code, places them as written here.)
- The typing field for an arrow's words (170 wide) opens where the words stand, aligned to the start (`BoardSession.wordsField(for:)`).
- Finding an arrow under a press (`hitLink`): the distance to the drawn line (curves as 12 short pieces), 9 screen points (the canvas uses 9 / scale; 8 board points in the rules' default).
- The painter keeps the arrows of the last board drawn (`ArrowCache`) and works them out again only when a shape's place, size or kind, a fold, or an arrow's ends, sides or style changed. Measured by the builder: a board of 300 shapes and 329 arrows (44 of them going round shapes) takes about 3.5 ms.
- The picture for PARAGON and for sharing is drawn by the same painter.

**Moving an end**

- A chosen arrow (not read-only, not while typing, not with the arrow tool on) shows a dot at each end: radius 5 screen points (shrinks with the zoom below 100 % like the other handles), plate colour with a 2-point green edge. A press within 13 screen points of a dot takes it (the far end first; `BoardSession.linkEnd(at:)`).
- While dragging: a dashed green line from the other end to the finger; the item the end would land on gets the dashed green frame.
- Let go (`Board.moveEnd(of:start:to:)`): on its own item, or within 30 points of it — the end moves to the side nearest the point (the box cut in four by its diagonals, `Route.nearestSide`) and that side is stored; if it is the side the app would find anyway, nothing is stored. On another item (not a frame) — the end moves there and its stored side is cleared. Refused, nothing changes: a frame, empty board, the item at the other end, or when the same arrow (same from and to) already exists. One undo step.
- Turn round swaps `from` and `to` and the two stored sides.

### 3.6 Hit-testing, press areas and carrying (BoardEdit.swift, Fingers.swift)

- `hit(point)`: items from the top: non-frames first (visible only). Diamond: |dx|/(w/2) + |dy|/(h/2) <= 1.02; oval: ellipse test with 1.03; idea and text: the box grown by 5 across and 4 up/down; others: the box. Frames after that, topmost first: caught only inside the top 30 points (name band), within 8 inside the edge, or up to 6 outside the edge; a lane's name band also catches a press on the frame.
- `Board.hit(_:scale:least:)` (new): the exact hit first (as `hit(_:)`); otherwise every visible non-frame thing smaller than `least / scale` in a direction gets a press area of that size; the nearest wins, ties to the topmost. Finger: `least 44`; pointer, Pencil, Mac: 0.
- `Fingers.button = 44`, `Fingers.control = 32`, `Fingers.close = 9` (screen points). `Fingers.control(at:boxes:onThing:least:close:)`: the handles (plus signs, size knob) as drawn, in screen points. Caught within 9 points of the box always; up to a 32-point press area only when no thing lies exactly under the press; the nearest wins. Finger: `least 32`; pointer, Pencil, Mac: `least 0` (9 points, as 0.1, but nearest instead of first). The look of the handles is unchanged.
- `hitLink(point, within:)`: the nearest arrow whose two ends are visible (3.5).
- `items(in: rect)` (the choosing box): every visible item the box touches; a frame only when the box wholly contains it.
- `carried(by:)` (what moves with a drag): a centre idea with all its descendants; a frame with every thing that BELONGS to it (3.12: its middle is inside; in 0.1 only what lay wholly inside); other ideas are left out (the map places them); other kinds alone.
- `duplicate`: copies the items (an idea with all ideas under it) 28 down and 28 right, with the arrows between them. A copied idea whose parent was not copied keeps its parent and takes `order + 0.5` (the next sister) and is not moved. `todo` is kept.
- `bounds`: the union of all visible items (nil when empty); for a timeline the box of its picture.

### 3.7 The canvas: view, gestures, undo

- View: `Viewport` (Core): `offset`, `scale`, the room `size`, the mark `movedByOwner` and (new) `pin`. Every way of moving the view — scroll, a finger slide on empty board (`slide(from:by:)`), two fingers (`pinch(from:to:by:)`), Space and a drag, `reveal`, zoom by button, key, pinch or ⌘-scroll — goes through `pan`, `zoom` or `pinch`, which set the mark. While the mark is not set the board is fitted again whenever its room changes (a window settling, the keyboard, a page over it); once it is set the view stays where the owner left it. Zoom range 0.2 – 3 (20 % – 300 %); buttons and ⌘+ / ⌘- change it by 1.25; zoom keeps the point under the pointer (or the view's middle). Fit: room = view minus 80 across and 170 down; scale = min(1, fit) but never below 0.5 (on a small screen with a large board the middle shows and the view is moved by hand); centred on the bounds; an empty board is shown at 100 % with the origin in the middle. A board is fitted the first time its size is known, unless a remembered view comes back (7.3). `reveal` moves the view only as far as needed to bring an item inside a safe rectangle (30 from the sides, 70 from the top, 120 from the bottom).
- Readable iPhone (`Viewport.resized(to:showing:readableAround:)`, iPhone only): with a main thing (`Board.mainThing` = the middle idea of the first map, else the first non-frame thing, else the first frame) the board opens with `open(_:around:least: 0.75)`: fit as before when the whole board fits at 75 % or more; otherwise scale 0.75 with the main thing in the middle, but the view never goes past the board's edge (margins: 40 left/right, 70 top, 100 bottom); a direction in which the board fits is centred. Not a move by the owner.
- Typing zoom (`BoardSession.typing(in:)`, called from `CanvasView.onChange(of: editing)`, iPhone only): `typingBegan(round:least: 1)` — when scale < 1 and not already zoomed for typing: remembers the scale (`scaleBeforeTyping`), zooms to 1 keeping the thing's middle where it is on screen, then `reveal`. `typingEnded(round:)` — back to the remembered scale round the thing last typed in, `reveal`, and the board opens again as it stood if the owner had not moved it. While zoomed for typing, a changed room (keyboard) does not re-fit. A zoom by the owner or "whole board" while typing forgets the remembered scale.
- Mac: scroll wheel / two fingers move the view (x8 for wheels without precise deltas); a magnify gesture zooms around the pointer; ⌘ + scroll zooms by 1 % per scroll unit; Space and a drag slide (2.6). The Mac keeps SwiftUI's `DragGesture` + `ScrollCatcher`.
- Touch layer (iPhone and iPad; `TouchLayer.swift`, `Press.swift`). `TouchLayer` (`UIViewRepresentable`) puts a clear `TouchSurface` (`UIView`) over the Canvas (`.overlay`, inside the `board.canvas` accessibility element, under the text field). On iOS the Canvas has no SwiftUI gesture any more. The surface swallows raw touches; all work is done by recognisers, all with `cancelsTouchesInView = false`, `delaysTouchesEnded = false`, delegate = the surface:
  - `fingerPress` — `UILongPressGestureRecognizer`, `minimumPressDuration = 0` (begins at touch-down), `allowedTouchTypes = [direct, indirectPointer]`.
  - `pencilPress` — the same, `allowedTouchTypes = [pencil]` (its own recogniser, so a resting hand never blocks it).
  - `twoPan` — `UIPanGestureRecognizer`, exactly 2 touches, `allowedTouchTypes = [direct]`, no scroll types.
  - `pinch` — `UIPinchGestureRecognizer`, `allowedTouchTypes = [direct]`; also receives a trackpad's pinch (no touches at `.began` → trackpad mode).
  - `scroll` — `UIPanGestureRecognizer`, `allowedTouchTypes = []`, `allowedScrollTypesMask = .all` (trackpad two-finger scroll and mouse wheel only).
  - `hover` — `UIHoverGestureRecognizer`: remembers where the pointer is.
  - `inkStroke` — `InkRecognizer` (own subclass): only touches the drawing hook claimed (3.15).
  - Simultaneous recognition: `true` between the surface's own recognisers; `true` with any `UIScreenEdgePanGestureRecognizer` (the page's edge swipe) — when that swipe begins the surface gives the press up; `false` with everything else (a press on the board wins).
  - Which touch goes where is decided once per touch (`gestureRecognizer(_:shouldReceive:)`, `verdict(for:)`): the first finger / pointer click → `fingerPress` (or `inkStroke` if claimed), a finger also to `twoPan` and `pinch`; a further finger while one is down → only `twoPan` + `pinch`, the running press is given up (`.lost(takeBack: true)`) and nothing more of it reaches the board until that finger lifts, a finger's ink stroke is cancelled; both fingers in the same moment → the press is ignored from its `.began`; Pencil → `pencilPress` (or `inkStroke` if claimed), a running finger press is given up, and fingers landing while the Pencil is down go to no recogniser.
  - `Press` (startLocation, location, translation, time, kind finger/pencil/pointer, shift) replaces `DragGesture.Value` in `CanvasView.begin/update/finish`; `pressMoved` / `pressEnded` are used by the Mac's `DragGesture` and by the layer. `PressPhase`: began, moved, ended, lost(takeBack).
  - One press, by kind: finger — 4-point slop, an idea lifts after 0.25 s, empty board slides; pointer — an idea lifts at once, empty board draws the choosing box, Shift from `modifierFlags` adds to the choice; Pencil — an idea lifts at once, empty board slides (until drawing claims it). Pointer and Pencil use exact press areas, a finger the finger-size ones (3.6).
  - Press lost with `takeBack` (`CanvasView.pressLost`): a running move or resize is ended with `liveEnd(keep:)`, `keep = !Fingers.isShove(moved:after:)`; **a shove = the second finger landed within 0.5 s of the first, or the thing moved less than 30 screen points** → the board goes back to where it was, no undo step, and `selection` / `selectedLink` are set back to what they were before the press landed. A slide of the view is kept. Lost without `takeBack` (system cancel): `resetPress()` (things stay as they stand).
  - Two fingers (`TouchSurface.step`): at every change the middle between the fingers and the pinch's scale since the last step go to `Viewport.pinch(from:to:by:)` — the board point under the old middle lies under the new middle, scale x factor clamped to 0.2…3; sets `movedByOwner`. With fewer than two fingers down the board rests; a jump of the middle of 150 points or more in one step re-bases instead of moving. No inertia.
  - Trackpad pinch: `zoom(to: scale x pinch.scale, around: pointer)`. Scroll: `pan(by: translation since last event)`; with ⌘: `zoom(to: scale x exp(clamp(dy, ±60) x 0.01), around: pointer)`. Pointer = last hover point, else the recogniser's location, else the middle of the view.
- Press and drag (a press that moves more than 4 points becomes a drag): on an item → move it (with all chosen items if it is chosen; with `carried(by:)`; falling in line, 3.11); on a non-centre idea → drag-an-idea (a ghost at 70 % follows; a dashed green frame marks an idea it would hang under, the green landing line a place among sisters, 3.4) — with a finger only when it rested at least 0.25 s before it moved (a light tap of the haptic motor marks the lift); a quicker slide that starts on an idea pans the view instead; on a plus → pull a line; on the size knob → resize; on an arrow's end dot → move the end (3.5); on the line between two lanes → move it (3.12); on an arrow or empty board → a choosing box with the Mac's pointer or an iPad's pointer, pan with a finger; in read-only mode a drag on an item pans. In arrow-tool mode a drag from an item to another joins them.
- Release of an idea drag: `Board.ideaDrop` decides (3.4): hang under an idea, another place among sisters, tear off (`detach` at the release point, and the notice `board.notice` "Made its own map" with the button `board.noticeUndo` "Undo" shows for 6 s; it goes at the next change, Undo or Redo), or spring back.
- Dragging ONE map by its centre: the dashed green frame marks the idea of another map under the pointer; released there, the map goes back to where it was and `reparent`s under that idea (the two maps become one), with the notice "Joined to the other map" and Undo. Released anywhere else it is an ordinary move.
- Keys (focus): the canvas takes the keyboard focus when a board opens, when typing ends, on every press on it, after every button of the tool pill, the selection bar, the colour row, the zoom and undo buttons, each item of the More menu, the rename and remove dialogs, and when the Send page closes (`BoardSession.takeKeys`).
- Release of a plus pull: from an idea → a new child; from another item → over another non-frame item joins them, else a new item is made with its middle at the landing point (3.11), joined, chosen and ready to type (kind as in 3.5).
- Press without moving: on a plus → idea: new child; other: `addNext` in that direction. On an item: chooses it (Shift with a pointer adds/removes). Two presses within 0.42 s on the same item or arrow → type its words. On empty board: chooses nothing; two presses within 0.42 s → on iPhone and iPad (`Finger.twoPressesFit`, true on iOS) the whole board is shown (`session.fit()`, eased 0.2 s); on the Mac a sticky note with its middle at the press, only when nothing was chosen (no item, no arrow) before the FIRST press. In arrow-tool mode: the first non-frame item sets the start, the second joins; a press on a frame is passed over and the tool stays on (a drag from an item and let go on a frame makes no arrow and ends the tool); empty board ends the tool. With `choosingMore` (iPhone/iPad) a press on an item adds or removes it like Shift on the Mac.
- Handles of a single chosen item (not while typing, not read-only, not in arrow-tool mode): idea centre: one plus, on the right; other idea: one plus on its side; free text: plus right and down; frame: size knob only (and the grips on its lane lines); every other kind: four plus signs and a size knob at the lower right corner. A plus is 18 points (screen) wide and stands 17 points from the item's edge. Handles keep that screen size when zoomed in and shrink with the zoom below 100 % (factor `min(1, max(scale, 0.55))`); their press area is in 3.6. The plus of a centre makes a child; it goes right in a one-sided map and to the emptier side in a both-sides map (3.4).
- Chosen items have a green outline (2 points, 5 / 4 outside the box).
- Undo: a stack of at most 120 earlier boards (the oldest falls away); a new change empties Redo. One drag, one typed item or one command is one step. Undo and Redo do nothing while typing. The stacks live only as long as the board stays open. When a newer version of the open board arrives from another device (`BoardSession.replace`), it takes the place of the open one and both stacks are emptied; while typing or dragging (`busy`) the open board is left alone and the news is held (2.2).
- `change` ignores a change that leaves the board equal; every kept change stamps `changed` and calls the save.
- Select all (⌘A): every visible item.
- Delete: removes the chosen items and arrow; if exactly one item was removed and it had a parent, the parent is chosen.
- To do: sets `todo` on every chosen item that is not a frame; if all chosen items already have it, clears it.
- Both sides: toggles `bothSides` on the centre of the chosen idea's map. Fold: toggles `folded` on the chosen idea.

### 3.8 Typing in place

- Press a tool, plus, chip or key that makes a thing → typing starts in it at once. Words go onto the board as typed (the board is re-fitted each keystroke). `commitEdit` trims the words. Every keystroke is also handed to the save (written 0.5 s later), so leaving the app mid-sentence loses nothing.
- Return: in an idea → ends and makes the next sister (`addSister`; for a centre, a first branch); in anything else → ends. Shift+Return or Option+Return → new line (hardware keyboard), in an idea too: the field makes the line where the cursor stands, and if it has not within 0.08 s the line is put at the end of the words. In an idea, exactly ONE new line break in the text without Shift (the screen keyboard's Return; `Typing.withoutNewBreak`) ends the idea and starts the next sister; if the idea is empty it just ends. Line breaks already in an idea stay, and several lines pasted in stay as lines. In lists and in PARAGON an idea with several lines is one line. Tab: in an idea → ends and makes a child; in a flow shape → keeps the words and makes the next shape (3.5); elsewhere → ends. Shift+Tab: in an idea → `moveOut` and the typing goes on; elsewhere nothing. ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ while an idea is typed on the Mac: the idea moves one place and the typing goes on (3.4). ⌘ + arrow key while a flow shape is typed: the next shape in that direction (so these keys do not move the cursor there). Escape → cancels (`cancelEdit`): the words go back to what they were when the typing began, and a thing made for this typing (tool, plus, Tab, Return) goes away again, its making taken back out of Undo, and its parent is chosen. Return, Done and pressing elsewhere on the canvas keep the words. Opening another board ends it.
- The keyboard bar (iPhone and iPad; `CanvasView.keyboardBar(isIdea:)` in `MapOrder.swift`, inside the field's `.keyboard` toolbar). For an idea: `board.key.under` (mark `.ideaUnder`; `commitEdit(then: .child)`), `board.key.next` (`.nextIdea`; `commitEdit(then: .sister)`), `board.key.out` (`.outLevel`; `moveOut`; switched off and 40 % when the idea's depth is below 2), a flexible space, `board.fieldDone` "Done". Each button: mark 17 in the mind-map colour + words (subheadline, medium), 30 high, 8 padding across, soft mind-map background, corner 8. Words: canvas at least 520 wide → "Idea under", "Next idea", "Out one level"; narrower → "Under", "Next", "Out". VoiceOver always reads the whole name. Anything else: a flexible space + "Done". An empty new idea + "Idea under" / "Next idea": the empty idea goes away and typing ends — the same as Tab / Return.
- The keyboard stays up: on iPhone and iPad all ideas are typed in ONE field (`CanvasView.fieldIdentity(isIdea:)` returns one fixed identity for ideas), so going from idea to idea never takes the field away. Other things, and everything on the Mac, get a fresh field per thing (`session.editing`).
- A new idea (made by the tool, plus, Tab, Return or Next idea) that ends empty, has a parent and no ideas under it, is removed again and its parent is chosen. A new centre left empty stays (it shows the faint word "Idea").
- A board still titled "New board" takes the first line of the only item that has words, cut to 40 characters.
- One typed item is one Undo step (adding it is another; the UI test relies on that: two undos reach "Empty"). A cancelled new thing leaves no step.
- Not typing, a chosen single item + any single printable character (no ⌃ or ⌥, no ⌘) starts typing and puts the character after the existing words.
- Words on an arrow: draft in a field 170 wide where the words stand (3.5). A lane's name: 3.12.

### 3.9 Keys (canvas, no typing in progress)

⌘A select all. Tab: child of the chosen idea; on one chosen flow shape the next shape in the flow's direction (3.5). Shift+Tab: the chosen idea moves out one level (`moveOut`, 3.4). ⌘ + arrow key on one chosen flow shape: the next shape in that direction. ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ on one chosen idea: one place up or down among its sisters (`shiftIdea`). Return: next idea beside a chosen idea (centre: first branch); other chosen item: type; chosen arrow: type. Delete / Forward Delete: remove. Escape: clear the choice (and `choosingMore`) and leave the arrow tool. Arrow keys: walk a chosen idea, else move chosen things (carried) 10 points, with Shift 40, without falling in line. Space: type in the chosen item (Mac: a short press; held with a drag it slides the board, 2.6). With one item chosen a printable character types (see 3.8). With no item chosen and no arrow chosen: m idea, b or r box, u round, d diamond, o oval, n note, t text, f frame, a or l toggles the arrow tool (these add at the middle of the view and end the arrow tool). With several things chosen the letters also add things. Menu keys (2.6): ⌘N, ⇧⌘N, ⌘↩, ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z, ⌘D, ⌘A, ⇧⌘T, ⇧⌘V, ⌘+, ⌘-, ⌘0 (Actual Size), ⌘1 (Show the Whole Board), ⌘↑, ⌘↓; ⌘V (Mac only). While words are typed on the board, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z go to the text field (Mac: the `undo:` / `redo:` actions; iPhone/iPad: the undo manager of the view that has the keys). Holding ⌘ while dragging on the Mac switches falling in line off (3.11). A timeline has its own keys (2.8).

### 3.10 Lists in and out (Outline.swift)

- `Outline.parse(text)`: one entry per line; blank lines ignored; text trimmed. Depth from leading spaces and tabs (a tab = 4 spaces; any consistent step; a smaller indent closes deeper levels). Markers stripped: one of `- * + • – ◦`, or a number with `.` or `)`, or a single letter with `)`, followed by a space; then a check box `[ ]`, `[x]`, `[X]`. A line that is only a marker is dropped. A Markdown heading (1–6 `#` then a space) has depth level − 1, and the following list lines nest under it (depth = heading depth + 1 + their indent steps; a new heading resets the indent steps). A line is pulled up so that it is at most one step deeper than the line before it.
- `Outline.isList` = at least two non-blank lines.
- `addMap(from:title:at:)`: one top node → it is the centre and its children are the main branches; several → the centre says the title or "Ideas" and the top nodes are the main branches. Rough sizes first (8.2 per character + 16, at most 260, at least 60; height 30; centre + 24 wide and 46 high), fitted by the app later. `bothSides` when there are more than 4 main branches. Branch colours follow 3.4.
- Paste (`BoardSession.paste`): ignored while typing, read-only, on a timeline, or with empty text. With exactly one idea chosen and a non-empty parse: the nodes hang under it (even one line). Else with `isList`: a new map with its middle at a free spot near the middle of the view (300 x 200 searched). Else (one line): a yellow sticky note with the text at a free spot. The new thing is chosen.
- `outlineText` (Copy the board as text): maps as `- ` bullets two spaces per level, separated by newlines; then "Flow:" with `A → B (words)` lines; "Notes:" with `- ` lines for notes and text; "Shapes:" with `- ` lines for shapes that no arrow touches. Sections are separated by a blank line. Line breaks inside words become a single space; empty words show as "(empty)". A timeline: its rows with their dates, then "Comes after:" lines. (**Note**: lanes are not mentioned in this text, and an arrow that is Both ways or No ends is still written with `→`.)

### 3.11 Falling in line, guides, dot grid, tidy (Snap.swift, DotGrid.swift, Tidy.swift; SnapShow.swift)

**Catching** (`Snap.move` / `Snap.resize`, `Board.moveSnapped` / `resizeSnapped` / `landing`)

- Catch distance: 6 screen points = 6 / zoom board points (`SnapShow.reach`). Reach 0 = no catch (⌘ held on the Mac, read from `NSEvent.modifierFlags` at every drag step; an iPad with a keyboard has no such key).
- What is moved is taken as ONE box (the box round all moved things; ideas hanging under a moved centre are not counted in it).
- Sideways: the box's left and right edge each catch a neighbour's left or right edge; its middle catches a neighbour's middle. Up-down the same with top, bottom, middle. Edges never catch middles.
- Equal gaps (neighbours in the same row = lying over the box up-down by more than the reach; column likewise): (a) between the nearest neighbour on each side, when there is room: the gap on both sides is the same; (b) after two neighbours in a row: the gap to the nearer one equals the gap between the two; (c) the same before two neighbours.
- Per direction the smallest correction within reach wins (on a tie: edges/middles before gaps). The two directions are independent.
- Targets (`Board.snapTargets`): every thing that is not moving itself, except ideas with a parent; folded-away things are none. A frame is a target by its 4 edges only (no middle, no equal gaps); a moved set with a frame in it does not use its middle either. What a dragged frame carries moves with it and is not a target.
- Sizing: only an edge that moved against the size at the start of the drag catches, and only neighbours' edges. If the smallest size or the words hold the edge back, the guide is not shown.
- Pulling out of a plus (`Board.landing`): the new shape (the kind's default size, middle at the finger) catches like a dragged thing; the middle of the shape it comes from wins over every other catch, so the arrow is one straight piece. No landing when the plus belongs to an idea (adds an idea) or the finger is over another thing that is not a frame (the two are joined).
- Not caught: ideas dragged inside a mind map; arrow keys (10 / 40 as before). One undo step per drag.

**Guides** (`SnapShow.swift`; held in `CanvasView.snap`; emptied when the press ends, is taken away by the system, or a pinch begins; drawn in `CanvasView` only, never by `BoardPainter`, so never in a picture)

- Colour `Paint.guide`: #E0457B by day, #F58FB2 by night; 1 screen point wide, round ends.
- Line guide: through everything in line, 10 screen points past the outermost things at both ends.
- Gap mark: a line across each equal gap at the middle of where the two things lie side by side, with a tick of 8 screen points (±4) at each end.
- Landing outline: dashed (5 on, 4 off), 1.5 screen points, corner 9, in the chosen-green (`Paint.chosen`).

**Dot grid** (`DotGrid.dots(in:scale:)`, drawn by `SnapShow.grid` straight after the background fill, under everything; never in a picture)

- A dot at every multiple of 24 board points (the board's zero point has one), 1.5 screen points wide at every zoom. Colour `Paint.dot`: #D5DAE2 by day, #2C2F36 by night.
- Thinning: the distance doubles (24 → 48 → 96) while it would be under 12 screen points: 24 from 50 % zoom up, 48 from 25 % to under 50 %, 96 below.
- Strength: full from 40 % zoom up, falling in a straight line to nothing at 20 %. Only dots inside the visible part are made.

**Tidy** (`Tidy.swift`; `BoardSession.lineUp() / evenGaps() / sameSize()`, each through `change` = one undo step; nothing happens while words are typed)

- `tidyable`: the chosen things without ideas that have a parent and without folded-away things.
- Row or column: a row when the middles are spread at least as much sideways as up-down.
- `lineUp` (2 or more): row → every middle to the average height of the middles; column → to the average sideways place. A map's centre takes its ideas along, a frame what belongs to it (unless that was chosen itself).
- `evenGaps` (3 or more): sorted by middle along the row/column; first and last stay; gap = (room between the outer two − sizes of the ones between) / (count − 1); may be negative when there is no room (they then overlap evenly).
- `sameSize` (2 or more of the eight shapes, note, frame): all take the width and height of the one with the largest area (tie: the wider); middles stay. Ideas and free text are left out. (If a shape's words need more height than the largest has, it grows again downward.)
- Buttons and menu lines: 2.3 and 2.6. Marks `Glyph.new(.lineUp / .evenGaps / .sameSize)`, tint `Part.shapes`.

### 3.12 Lanes and belonging (Lanes.swift; LanesView.swift)

- Stored: the optional field `lanes` on a frame item (3.2). `cuts` are the lines between lanes as parts of the lanes' room, rising, one fewer than names. Missing = a plain frame.
- 2 to 5 lanes, as rows or as columns. The frame's own name band (30 high) stays above the lanes; the lanes share the rest. Name band of a lane: 24 wide at the left of a row, 24 high at the top of a column.
- Giving a frame lanes, or another number: names that fit stay, the lanes are even again, and a frame too small grows (rows: height at least 30 + 60 x lanes; columns: width at least 60 x lanes). Another direction keeps all names. "No lanes" removes the field.
- Dragging a line: both lanes beside it keep at least 60; the line stops there. Sizing the frame: never smaller than 120 x 80, and never so small that the narrowest lane falls under 60. The lanes stretch with the frame.
- Which lane holds a point (`Lanes.index(at:in:)`): by its place along the lanes; a point on a line counts for the lane after it; a point in the frame's name band counts for the first row (or the column under it).
- Belonging (`Board.frame(_:holds:)`): a thing belongs to a frame when its MIDDLE is inside — for every frame, with or without lanes. A frame belongs to another frame only when it lies wholly inside. Of a mind map only the centre counts; its ideas travel with it. Moving a frame (drag, arrow keys, Line up …) carries what belongs to it (`carried(by:)`); sizing carries nothing. `spot(of:)`: the frame and lane of a thing — the smallest frame round its middle when frames lie in each other.
- On the board (`LanesView.swift`): drawn on the frame, under everything in it: name bands tinted with the frame's colour (13 % by day, 20 % by night), lines 1.1 wide at 60 % of the colour, names 12 semibold in the colour — upright (bottom to top) in a row's band, level in a column's band; a name too long for its band ends in "…". An unnamed lane shows "Lane 1", "Lane 2" … faintly on the live board; never in a picture.
- Light while dragging (`SnapShow.over` → `BoardPainter.frames`): the lane under the middle of each dragged thing is filled with 17 % (night 24 %) of the frame's colour and the frame's outline is 2.6 wide; a frame without lanes is filled with 14 % (night 20 %). A frame that is dragged itself does not light up. Never in a picture. (**Note**: pulling a new shape out of a plus does not light a lane.)
- Lines of a chosen frame: a grip (34 x 5, the frame's colour) in the middle of each line (`lanes.line.<n>`); the whole line takes a drag, 7 points to each side. One Undo step per drag. On the Mac the pointer becomes a resize arrow.
- A lane's name: two presses on its name band. The field `lanes.nameField` lies level (a row: 170 wide at the top of its band; a column: as wide as the column, 110 to 220). Return or Done keeps, Tab keeps and goes to the next lane, Escape leaves it, a press elsewhere keeps. One Undo step per name. (**Note**: ⌘Z while a lane's name is typed undoes a board step, not the typing.)
- The plate (`plate.lanes`, opened by `sel.lanes`): heading "Lanes" with Cancel; "No lanes" (`lanes.none`), and for Rows and for Columns the numbers 2 3 4 5 (`lanes.rows.2` … `lanes.rows.5`, `lanes.columns.2` … `lanes.columns.5`); what the frame has now is in full colour; a line of help: "Press a lane's name twice to type it. Drag the line between two lanes to move it."; one Undo step per pick.

### 3.13 Timeline rules (Timeline.swift)

- `Day`: a calendar day as a whole number (days since 1 Jan 1970), from/to ISO text, weekday (Monday = 0; weeks start on Monday), week start, month start, "1 Dec", "1 Dec 2026". No clock and no time zone in it.
- `TimeScale`: days 28 points a day, weeks 8 a day (a week is 56), months 2.4 a day (a month is about 72). New bar length: 3 / 7 / 30 days. Air either side: 3 / 7 / 21 days. `fitting(days:width:)` = the closest scale at which the days fit.
- `TimelineGeometry`: `x(of: day)`, `day(atX:)`, `days(forDrag:)` (rounds to the nearest whole day), `y(ofRow:)`, `row(atY:)`, `bar(from:to:row:)` (1 in from each day edge, at least 4 wide), `diamond(on:row:)` (middle of the day, side = bar height − 2), `todayX` (middle of today), `ticks` (days: every day's number, Mondays strong; weeks: every Monday "1 Dec"; months: every 1st "Dec"), `heads` (the small words above: month + year over days, the year over weeks and months), `arrow(from:to:)`.
- Arrow: out 8 from the end of the forerunner, down (or up) to the follower's row, in to its start. When the follower starts less than 14 to the right, the arrow runs back along the line between the two rows (6 points). Corners rounded with radius 4, line 1.4, a small filled head. "Comes after" is an ordinary `Link` (`from` = the forerunner, `to` = the follower).
- Rows: `timelineRows` = all bars, milestones and phases by `row` (ties in the order made). `moveRow(id, to:)` renumbers 0…n−1. One thing per row. `x`, `y`, `w`, `h` of timeline items are not used.
- `shift(ids, byDays:)`, `stretch(id, .start/.end, to:)`, `setDates(id, start:, end:)` — each returns how many OTHER bars were pushed.
- `settle`: (1) a moved bar that now starts before the end of a bar it comes after is put on the day after that end; (2) every follower that starts on or before its forerunner's last day is moved to the day after it, keeping its length, and so on down the chain. Bars are only pushed later, never pulled earlier.
- `stretch(.start)`: never past its own end, never before the day after its forerunners' latest end. `stretch(.end)`: never before its own start; pushes followers. A bar is whole days and never shorter than 1 day.
- `comesAfter(follower, after: forerunner)`: refused for a phase, for the same pair twice, and when it would close a ring (`leads(from:to:)`).
- `addBarAfter(id)`: the row below, starts the day after, as long as the source (7 days after a milestone), the source's colour, joined.
- `duplicateRows`: the copy goes into the row under its original; arrows are not copied.
- `phaseGroups`: a phase holds every row under it down to the next phase; its days = first start … last end of its bars. (**Note**: dragging a phase's name moves the phase alone; its rows do not travel with it.)
- `timelineRange(today:air:)`: first bar's start − air … last bar's end + air, widened to hold today.
- `timelinePicture()`: the scale that fits 1100 points, starts on a Monday (months: on the 1st), names column 120–260 by the longest name.
- New bar colours: taken in turn from the 7 branch colours; milestone purple; phases in turn teal, blue, orange, green, pink, purple.
- `countLine` and "Copy the board as text": 2.2 and 3.10. To PARAGON: 5.9.

### 3.14 Search, "Changed …", small pictures (Home.swift; BoardThumb.swift)

- Search (`BoardSearch.find(_ query:in:)` / `Board.holds(_:)`): the query is cut into words at spaces; a board is found when EVERY word is contained in `readableText` = the title + every item's text + every arrow's text. Compared without case and without accents; a word may be part of a longer word. An empty query finds every board; the order of the boards is kept.
- "Changed …" (`Board.changedWords(now:calendar:)` = "Changed " + `Board.ago(from:to:calendar:)`): under 1 minute or ahead of now → "just now"; under 60 minutes → "1 minute ago" / "N minutes ago"; same calendar day → "1 hour ago" / "N hours ago"; 1 day → "yesterday"; 2–6 days → "N days ago"; 7–13 → "last week"; 14–59 → "N weeks ago"; 60–364 → "N months ago" (days / 30); then "a year ago" / "N years ago".
- Small pictures (`Thumbs`): a board is drawn with `BoardPainter` (no handles; a timeline by `TimelinePainter`) into 240 x 150 points at 2x (480 x 300 pixels) on `Paint.canvas`, the whole board centred with 12/10 room, never bigger than 60 % of its real size. Lettering is fitted first, as for the PARAGON picture. Kept in memory only (`NSCache`, at most 160 pictures or 48 MB). Key: the board's identity (or `starter.<rawValue>`) + day/night; the entry carries the board's `changed` time — a different time means a new picture, which replaces the old one. `Thumbs.drawn` counts real drawings. `BoardThumb` (the view) reads the kept picture while drawing the page; when there is none it draws it in a `.task` after the page is up and shows the previous picture meanwhile. Hidden from VoiceOver.

### 3.15 Pencil drawing (Ink.swift, InkDraw.swift, InkDesk.swift, InkViews.swift)

Pick 2 of the panel. Drawing by hand: lines drawn with the Pencil, a finger or the mouse, kept as a "drawing" (a sketch).

**Stored (format 2, the number is not changed)**
- New kind word `drawing` (`ItemKind.drawing`, word "Sketch", `countLine` "1 sketch" / "n sketches").
- New item field `strokes` (an optional list, written only for a drawing). Each stroke: `colour` (a palette name; "" = plain ink), `width` ("thin" | "medium" | "thick"; another word is drawn medium and written back as it was), `points` (a flat list x0, y0, x1, y1 ... from the drawing's corner, rounded to 0.1), `pressure` (optional; one number 0 to 1 per point, rounded to 0.01; only when the Pencil drew it). Unknown fields inside a stroke are carried. A list with an odd number of numbers loses its last one; a pressure list of the wrong length is dropped.
- `StoredFieldsTests` golden list for format 2: `strokes` added to the item names. The stroke's own names are pinned in `InkTests.testADrawingGoesToTheFileAndBackAndAnOlderFileStillOpens`.
- An older app: the kind is unknown and the format is 2, so it is read-only, with the kind word and `strokes` written back untouched (the rule of 3.2).
- On the device only (UserDefaults, not iCloud, not used with `-sampleData`): `ink.width`, `ink.colour`.
- No `text`, no tick: `beginEdit` refuses a drawing; `toggleTodo` leaves drawings out; the To do, Colour and Words chips are not offered (Duplicate, Choose more, Remove only; 2.9).

**Rules (`Core/Sources/SeedboardCore/Ink.swift`, all pure)**
- `InkWidth`: thin 2, medium 4, thick 7.5 board points.
- Box: the box of all points (rounded to 0.1) with `Ink.margin` = 6 round it; a dot is 12 x 12. `Board.addStroke(points:pressure:colour:width:to:)` makes a new drawing on top of everything or grows the given one (older strokes stay where they were on the board). Points that are not numbers are dropped; a press that went nowhere is one point.
- Thinning: `Ink.thin`, Ramer-Douglas-Peucker with pressure as a third direction (0.1 of pressure weighs 0.3 points). Tolerance `Ink.tolerance(scale)` = 0.4 screen points, at most 0.5 board points. (A made-up floor plan: 11 lines, 140 points, the whole board file 4.3 kB.)
- Joining: `Ink.joins`: the new stroke began at most 5 s (`joinSeconds`) after the last stroke ended AND its box is at most 40 points (`joinReach`) from the box of the drawing that stroke went into (`Board.drawingToJoin`; the drawing must still be there). The time is kept by the open board (`InkDesk.lastDrawing`), not stored.
- Resizing: `Board.resize` -> `scaleDrawing`: the lines stretch with the box, thickness stays, margin stays 6, never less than 4 points of drawing a side, a flat line stays flat. "Same size" leaves drawings out (`sizeable`).
- Pressing: `Board.hit` exact = within 2 board points of a line's edge; `hit(_:scale:least:)` = within `max(least / 2, 6)` screen points (finger 22, mouse / Pencil 6) of a line's edge, topmost drawing first, before the small-thing rule; never the empty inside of the box. The choosing box takes a drawing by its box.
- Rubber: `Board.strokes(near:from:reach:)`: every stroke within `Ink.rubber` = 10 screen points of the rubber's way since the last sample (so a quick rub cannot jump over a line); `removeStrokes`: whole strokes go, the box shrinks to what is left, an empty drawing is removed with its arrows.
- Width under pressure: `Ink.width(base, pressure)` = base x (0.65 + 0.7 x min(p / 0.6, 1)^0.7): 0 gives -35 %, about 0.25 gives the chosen width, 0.6 and more gives +35 %. No pressure: the chosen width. (The curve is a first guess.)
- The drawn line: `Ink.runs`: straight pieces, every corner rounded by a quadratic curve whose ends lie on the two pieces and whose control point is the corner. The curve's reach along each piece = min(half the shorter piece, 2 x 0.6 / sin(half the turn)): a gentle bend is rounded mid-point to mid-point, a sharp corner stays within 0.6 points of the drawn corner. With pressure the line is cut into runs of one width, in steps of max(0.2, 6 % of the chosen width); a long straight piece whose pressure changes is cut so no step shows.
- Moved, copied, framed, snapped, lined up, removed and undone like a note; arrows can be joined to a sketch (it works like a note there). `countLine` counts sketches ("2 notes · 3 sketches").
- PARAGON: `noteBullets` adds "- A sketch (see the picture)" in board order ("(on the board)" when no picture goes along); `ParagonExport.label` of a drawing = "A sketch" (also for an arrow joined to one; Flow then says "A sketch"). A sketch has no tick and never becomes a task.
- The board's small picture on the home page and the PARAGON picture show sketches (one painter).

**Painting (`App/Sources/InkDraw.swift`)**: `InkDraw.stroke` paints the runs with round ends and round joins; `BoardPainter.drawInk` paints a drawing (called from `drawItem`), so the live board, the Mac, the home pictures and the PARAGON picture are one code. Colour: `InkDraw.colour` = `paint.line(name)`, "" = `paint.ink` (dark by day, light by night). A chosen drawing has the usual green outline and only the size knob (`handles`).

**The hand (`App/Sources/InkDesk.swift`)**
- `BoardSession.ink` (`InkDesk`): `on` (Draw tool), `rubber`, `width`, `colour`, `wet` (`WetLine`).
- `inkClaims` (TouchLayer's `InkHook.claims`, once per touch): Pencil: with Draw on always; with Draw off not while the arrow tool is on, and only where `inkBare`: no arrow end, no handle of the one chosen thing, no thing (drawings that are not chosen are left out), no arrow within 9 screen points. Finger: only with Draw on and not `InkDesk.pencilOnly` (iPad idiom and `UIPencilInteraction.prefersPencilOnlyDrawing`). Pointer (iPad mouse / trackpad): only with Draw on. Never on a read-only board, a timeline or a Who does what board. A stroke claimed by `ink` never reaches `pressed`, so drawing and press-and-hold (2.9) do not meet.
- `inkBegan`: ends typing, clears the choice, takes the keys; pen: `wet.begin`; rubber: `liveStart` + first rub. `inkMoved(real, predicted)`: all in-between samples are added, the predicted ones are drawn and replaced each time. `inkEnded(cancelled)`: pen: thin, join or new, `change { addStroke }` = one Undo step; rubber: `liveEnd(keep:)` = one Undo step; cancelled (second finger, system): nothing kept. A second finger cancels a finger's stroke; fingers never cancel a Pencil stroke, and a hand resting on the glass is ignored also when it lands before the Pencil.
- A Pencil tap with no tool on (moved at most 4 screen points, at most 0.35 s) on a drawing's line (6 screen points) chooses that drawing instead of leaving a dot. Elsewhere a tap leaves a dot. The Pencil over a drawing that is not chosen draws (one line in `inkBare` brings the strict rule "the Pencil chooses or moves a thing" back).
- Mac: `inkMouse` in `CanvasView.dragGesture`: a drag that begins while Draw is on is a line (or the rubber), all the way to its end.
- Leaving the tool (again, Done, Escape, the arrow tool, a thing that starts typing) also takes the pen again.
- Pencil double-tap / squeeze (`pencilTaps` on the touch layer): iOS 17.5 and later SwiftUI `onPencilDoubleTap` / `onPencilSqueeze` (at `.ended`) with `preferredPencilDoubleTapAction` / `preferredPencilSqueezeAction`; before 17.5 a `UIPencilInteraction` (double-tap only). switchEraser / switchPrevious: swap pen and rubber; showColorPalette / showInkAttributes / showContextualPalette: Draw tool on / off (so fingers draw too until it is left); ignore / runSystemShortcut: nothing.
- Scribble: nothing was built; the place where words are typed in a box, note or idea is an ordinary text field.

**Wet line (`InkLayer` in `InkViews.swift`)**: a second, light `Canvas` over the board (under the text field, no presses, hidden from VoiceOver). Only it listens to `WetLine`; a sample repaints the one running line (the same `InkDraw.stroke`, real + predicted points); the board's own Canvas, `Lettering.fit` and the map layout are not touched until the lift. The rubber shows a ring of 10 screen points.

**Screens (`InkViews.swift`)**
- `DrawTool` in `ToolPill` after Frame: `tool.draw` (the 8th tool; the row is 8 tools = 374 points wide, and scrolls sideways on an iPhone narrower than 394 points), 40 x 40 like the other tools, part colour `Part.drawing` (new: day #BF2F52, night #F2879C), full colour while on, value "On" / "Off". No letter key on the Mac ("D" is the diamond), and no Insert menu line.
- `DrawPlate` above the tools row (`draw.plate`): `draw.pen`, `draw.rubber`, `draw.thin`, `draw.medium`, `draw.thick` (tiles of `Finger.shape`: 36 x 36 iPhone / iPad, 30 x 28 Mac), dots `draw.colour.ink` and `draw.colour.<palette name>` (26 points iPhone / iPad, 22 Mac; a ring round the one in use), `draw.done` (full colour). It wraps onto more rows when narrow. `draw.pencilOnly` (the line about the Settings switch), `draw.rubberLine` + `draw.backToPen` (tool off, Pencil rubbing: "The Pencil rubs out").
- Marks used: `Glyph.new(.draw / .rubber / .thin / .medium / .thick)`.
- Searching on the home page cannot find a sketch (it has no words), and `-pick` cannot pick one.

**Not built**: Pencil hover; the Pencil's later, more exact pressure values (the first values are used); a lasso, changing the colour of a line afterwards; a stored picture of a large sketch (it is repainted whole on every slide of the board, like everything else on it); "Copy the board as text" does not mention sketches.

**Tests**: `InkTests.swift`, 11 data tests (thinning; box and growing; joining; scaling; pressing a line; rubber; file round trip + older file + newer stroke; moved / copied / framed / snapped / removed; the drawn line (corner and bend); pressure; PARAGON line). `UITests/DrawUITests.swift` (2, written and compiled, not run): blank board -> `tool.draw` -> plate buttons there -> thick, red -> drag on `board.canvas` -> `board.count` "1 sketch" -> Undo "Empty" -> Redo -> Rubber, drag across -> "Empty" -> Done, plate gone; `-board Sketch` -> "2 notes · 3 sketches".

### 3.16 Sequence diagram, "Who does what" (Sequence.swift, SequenceDraw.swift, SequenceView.swift, SequenceSession.swift, SequenceBars.swift)

Pick 3 of the panel. A board whose names stand in a row with a dashed line down, and whose messages are level arrows that carry a place in the order. The app places everything; no place is stored for the things.

**Stored (format 2, the number is not changed)**
- Board: `kind = "sequence"` (`Board.sequenceKind`, `Board.isSequence`). New: `Board.placesItself` = timeline or sequence.
- New item kinds (`ItemKind`): `who` (word "Who"), `message` ("Message"), `band` ("Note"), `section` ("Section"). `isStep` = message, band, section; `isSequence` = those and who.

**Rules (`Core/Sources/SeedboardCore/Sequence.swift`)**

- `wouldAnswer(from:to:at:)`: true when the step right above is a message the other way between the same two names and not an answer itself. Used for pulled and "Message to..." messages.
- `addMessageAfter(id)` (Return): right under it, from / to swapped, answer = the chosen one was not an answer (never for a message to itself).
- `moveStep(id, to:)`, `moveWho(id, to:)`: place counted without the moved thing; true when the place changed.
- `turnMessageRound(id)`: swaps from and to.
- `removeFromSequence(ids)`: a name takes every message from or to it; returns how many messages went along that were not chosen themselves. `messageCount(of:)`.
- `duplicateInSequence(ids)`: a step's copy right under it; a name's copy right beside it (no messages).
- `sequenceCountLine`: "4 people · 9 messages · 1 note · 1 section" ("Empty" for none; singular "1 person", "1 message").
- `stepLine(id)`: "2. Bank answers Anna: Yes"; `sequenceText` (Copy the board as text): "Who: Anna, Bank, Builder", then the numbered lines, "   Note: ...", "<section>:".
- Search on the home page finds names and words (they are ordinary item texts).

**Layout (`SequenceMetrics`, `SequenceLayout`; board points, the diagram's box starts at 0,0)**

## 5. Send to PARAGON (Paragon.swift, ParagonGate.swift, SendSheet.swift)

Two rules protect the owner's notes: Seedboard only ever adds new files to PARAGON's folder (it never opens, changes or replaces an existing one; files are written without overwriting; the one file it may take away again is a picture it made itself a moment ago in the same hand-over, when the note for it could not be written), and Inbox lines are never written into `Inbox.md` but into the waiting list that PARAGON's own Share button uses.

### 5.1 Finding PARAGON

- PARAGON's notes folder: the `Documents` folder of the iCloud container `iCloud.com.schabbauer.AMSPara`. It counts as PARAGON's folder IN USE when it holds `Inbox.md` (or `.Inbox.md.icloud`) or PARAGON's own settings file `.ams-para/config.json` (or `.ams-para/.config.json.icloud`) — `ParagonExport.looksLikeParagonFolder`. A folder with only a `Projects` folder in it is not taken: that can be a folder PARAGON never used. (Seedboard cannot ask PARAGON which folder it uses when the owner pointed PARAGON at a folder of his own; only this container is looked at.)
- The Inbox waiting list: the file `capture-outbox.jsonl` in the app-group container `group.com.schabbauer.amspara` (macOS: `D24ENP83QQ.group.com.schabbauer.amspara`). If the group container cannot be reached, notes and projects can still be sent and the Inbox cannot.
- Without iCloud (no container) PARAGON is "not found". With `-sampleData` PARAGON is "not found" (unless `-paragonFolder`, below).
- Looking is slow the first time and runs off the main thread; it runs at launch, on Settings → Check again, and again at EVERY Send (the folder may have appeared, gone or changed since the app started); the PARAGON line in Settings follows what the last look found.
- Entitlements: sandbox, user-selected files read-write, iCloud documents with both containers (Seedboard's own first, PARAGON's second), and the app group above (iPhone/iPad: `project.yml`; Mac: `App/Config/AMSSeedboard-macOS.entitlements`). `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`: `amspara`.

### 5.2 Names

- Note title (`noteTitle`): `/ \ : * ? " < > |`, line breaks, tabs and control characters become a space; spaces squeezed; dots and spaces trimmed at both ends; at most 100 characters (re-trimmed); `(conflict` becomes `(clash` (PARAGON reads "(conflict …)" as a conflict copy); an empty title becomes "Board"; the words "capture" and "timeblock" (any case) become "<word> (board)".
- Free title (`freeTitle`): compared with every `.md` file name in the PARAGON folder (all sub-folders, hidden folders skipped, `.icloud` placeholders counted), case-insensitive. If taken: "Title (board 2)", "(board 3)"… A name that would look like "Name 2" (a single digit 2–9 after a space) beside an existing "Name" is also treated as taken.
- Picture name: `Seedboard <safe title, at most 60 characters> <yyyy-MM-dd HHmm>.png` (letters, digits, space, `- _ . ,` kept; others become spaces); empty → "board". If it exists: " b2", " b3"… (up to 50 tries).

### 5.3 What is written where (all relative to PARAGON's folder)

- As a note: `Resources/<Title>.md`, and `Pictures/<picture>.png`.
- As a project: `Projects/<Title>.md`, and `Pictures/<picture>.png`.
- The picture is written first; if it fails nothing else is written and the page says "The picture could not be saved in PARAGON's folder. Nothing was changed." If the note then fails, the picture made a moment ago (only that new file) is taken away again and the page says "The note could not be saved in PARAGON's folder. Nothing was changed."; if the picture cannot be taken away either: "The note could not be saved in PARAGON's folder. Its picture “<name>” is left in PARAGON's Pictures folder; no note uses it."  The picture is made from the board in the day look on a white background (the plates behind words on arrows are white too), the bounds grown by 40, at 2x (1x when the larger side is over 3000 points). Nothing is picture-written for "Only the to-do boxes". An empty board has no picture (and the Send page does not allow it).
- Only the to-do boxes: no file in the folder; lines go to the waiting list.
- The waiting list is JSON Lines: one line per entry, appended in one step (`O_APPEND`), so PARAGON and Seedboard cannot write into each other. Each line: `id` (new UUID), `text`, `target` (inbox), `asTask` (true for a task, false for a note line), `createdAt` (ISO 8601; task lines are 1 s apart in board order). The lines are made in SeedboardCore (`ParagonExport.WaitingLine`, `taskLines`, `noteLine`, `waitingData`), and a test reads them back with a copy of PARAGON's own reader (`CaptureItem`, PARAGON build 339). All the lines of one send are written in ONE append: either every task is on its way, or none (a failed write says the Inbox cannot be reached). PARAGON files the lines itself the next time it opens.
- As a note: after the note is written, one line `[[<Title>]] — a board from Seedboard` with `asTask` false is added, only when the waiting list is reachable (a failure to write it is ignored). The result message: "“<Title>” is in PARAGON under Resources, with a line in the Inbox." or "… under Resources." or "… under Projects."
- Only the to-do boxes: one task per unsent to-do item that has words, in board order. So that the same tasks can never go twice, the "already sent" ticks are saved FIRST: the board with the ticks is written to the device (and waited for) before the lines are added to the waiting list; if adding them fails, the board is stored again without the ticks. A second Send while one is on its way is refused ("The board is on its way already."). Message: "1 task is on the way to the PARAGON Inbox. They show the next time PARAGON opens." (or "N tasks are…"). Errors: "No to-do boxes are waiting to be sent." / "This device cannot reach PARAGON's Inbox. Send the board as a note or a project instead."
- Afterwards (`markSent`): for a note or project the board's `sent` is set (when, kind, title, path "<Folder>/<Title>.md") and EVERY to-do item (also ones whose words are empty) is added to `sentTodos`; for to-do boxes only the sent ones are added. The board is saved at once. Taking the To do off a box (`toggleTodo`, all chosen ticked → cleared) removes it from `sentTodos`, so ticking it again sends it again. Copies of a board start with `sentTodos` empty.
- Open in PARAGON: opens `amspara://<title, percent-encoded>`.
- Board link: `seedboard://board/<board UUID>` (what happens when it arrives: 2.2). In PARAGON it opens from the reading view, and since PARAGON build 339 also while the note is edited (iPhone and iPad: a tap on its words; Mac: ⌘-click).

A value that starts with `[ { " '` in the front matter is quoted. A section with no lines is left out. The picture line is left out when there is no picture. ### 5.6 A project by the ticks (`ProjectPlan.swift`)

- `ParagonExport.ticksDecide(board)`: true when at least one *idea* has `todo`. False → the text is exactly as in 5.5.
- True:
  - a ticked centre adds `- [ ] <centre>` before everything else (it is still the Outcome);
  - an unticked main branch with ideas under it → `### <branch>`, then its ideas; with none → a plain `- <branch>` before the headings;
  - a ticked main branch → `- [ ] <branch>` before the headings, its ideas indented under it;
  - every idea below: ticked → `- [ ] <words>`, unticked → `- <words>`;
  - indent (two spaces per level): a plain line stands one level below the line of the idea it hangs under; a task stands one level below the nearest *ticked* idea above it in the branch, and at the left edge when there is none. Reason: PARAGON hangs an indented task under the nearest task above it with less indent and passes over plain lines (`TaskParser.parse(text:)`), so a ticked idea under an unticked one would otherwise become a sub-task of an unrelated task;
  - `### Steps`: every shape with words in arrow order, ticked → `- [ ]`, unticked → `- `;
  - `### To do`: ticked notes and free text, as before.
- Example: the sample board "Summer cabin" gave 13 tasks in 0.1; with its two ticks it now gives 2 tasks ("Permit", "Check timber prices in May") and 11 plain lines.
- `ParagonExport.projectTally(board:)` → `ProjectTally { ticks, ideaTasks, ideaPlain, shapeTasks, shapePlain, otherTasks, anyTick }`, counted from the very lines `project()` writes.
- `ParagonExport.projectLine(board:)` (the Send page's line `send.projectLine`, 2.4): when the ticks decide: "N ticked ideas become tasks" ("things" instead of "ideas" when a ticked shape, note or text is among them; "1 ticked idea becomes a task"), then ", M stay as plain lines" (", 1 stays as a plain line"; left out when 0), full stop. Otherwise: "Nothing is ticked: all N ideas become tasks." / "… all N ideas and M shapes become tasks." / "… the 1 idea becomes a task."; it starts "No idea is ticked:" when a note, text or shape is ticked but no idea; no line when the project would hold neither ideas nor shapes.

### 5.8 Lanes (`ParagonLanes.swift`)

- As a note: after "Flow", per frame with lanes `## <frame name>` ("Lanes" when it has none), then per lane that holds something with words `### <lane name>` and its things as `- ` lines, in reading order (rows: left to right; columns: top to bottom). Empty lanes and frames with nothing in them are left out. Sticky notes and free text in a lane are listed there and also under "Notes" as before.
- As a project: per lane a task group `### <frame name>: <lane name>` (only `### <lane name>` when the frame has no name) with the lane's flow shapes as `- [ ]` tasks, placed before "### Steps"; those shapes are not repeated under "Steps". When the ticks decide (5.6) the lane groups are part of the same plan.

### 5.9 A timeline board (`Timeline.swift`, hooked into `Paragon.swift`)

- PARAGON's task line (read-only look at PARAGON build 339: `TaskParser.swift`, `Note.swift`) carries one day of its own: ` >2026-12-14` (PARAGON's "due"; the mark must stand alone between spaces or at the line's end). There is **no start or defer day** on a task. `@deadline(…)` exists, but PARAGON counts a task overdue from the earlier of the two, so "do on the first day, in by the last" would turn a running bar red on its second day. Therefore the bar's **last day** is written in PARAGON's own form and the first day in plain words.
- Task line: `- [ ] Foundation (starts 8 Dec) >2026-12-12`. The year is added to the words when start and end are in different years: `(starts 28 Dec 2026) >2027-01-06`. A one-day bar and a milestone: `- [ ] Roof on >2027-02-20`.
- As a project: front matter as in 5.5 plus `due: <last day on the timeline>` (PARAGON reads a project's own due date from that line); `## Outcome` = the title; `## Tasks` = the rows above the first phase, then `### <phase>` with its tasks, each group in date order (first day, then last day); `## Notes` = "- Walls comes after Foundation" lines; `## Log`.
- As a note: `## Timeline` (rows with dates, e.g. "Permit: 1 Dec – 5 Dec 2026 · 5 days"; a phase bold, its rows indented; "(milestone)" after a milestone's name), `## Comes after`, `## Tasks` (rows marked To do, as task lines with their dates). The picture is the timeline (`timelinePicture()`, 3.13).
- Only the to-do boxes: unchanged (`inboxTasks`): the rows marked To do go to the Inbox.
- Seedboard still only adds new files.

## 6. Storage and safety (BoardStore.swift; the decisions in SeedboardCore, Safety.swift)

- One file per board: `<UUID>.seedboard` (JSON, 3.2). Folders (device, inside the app's sandbox): Application Support/`Boards`, `Removed`, `SafetyCopies`. iCloud (container `iCloud.com.schabbauer.AMSSeedboard`, document scope public, name "Seedboard"): `Documents/Boards`, `Documents/Removed`. The cloud folder is asked for again every 30 s until found, so signing in later is noticed.
- Save: never makes the app wait, and the device never waits for iCloud. `BoardStore.save` hands the board to `BoardDisk.save`, which has two lines of work, each doing one thing at a time: `local` writes the device file at once (atomically), `cloud` does everything in iCloud's folder through file coordination. Several quick saves of one board become one iCloud write (the newest waiting version). `flush()` waits for the device line (2 s at most) when the app is left; iCloud's line gets background time (2.2). `cloudIsOn` is false without an iCloud account or in sample mode; the app then works from the device copy alone, and Settings says "iCloud is off" (2.5).
- What a board "grew from" (`Lineage`): for each board the store remembers the `changed` stamps of every version the app SHOWED (at loading, or news it took) and every version it saved, the newest 600. Two stamps less than 1.5 thousandths of a second apart are the same version (files keep thousandths).
- The rules:
  1. For the same board the newer `changed` wins; a losing version that differs is kept in `SafetyCopies` as `<UUID>-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>-<6 letters>.seedboard` (these single set-aside files are not removed by the 14-day rule but deleted after 30 days, 6.1; the same bytes are never kept twice). New in 0.2, in the NAME of a set-aside file only: the ending `-here` (a version this device showed or saved) or `-away` (a version another device wrote that was never shown here) before `.seedboard`; no ending = not known (the board had not been shown in this run of the app yet; all files of 0.1). Decided in `BoardDisk.setAside` from the lineage. Before a save writes over a stored version (on the device or in iCloud) that is NOT one this board grew from — another device wrote it and this app never showed it — that version is kept in `SafetyCopies` first (`SafetyRules.write`: write / keep the stored one then write / the stored one is newer and stays, mine is kept / nothing / refuse).
  2. A version that iCloud parked because two devices wrote at once (`NSFileVersion`) is settled BY ITSELF and only when it was really read and its words are safe: the newer wins (its own bytes go into the board's file), the other is kept in `SafetyCopies`; one that cannot be read as a board is kept there as `….unreadable`. A parked version that has not arrived, or cannot be dealt with now, stays for the next look. iCloud's own copies `<UUID> 2.seedboard` (any number) are read too and joined by the same rule (the loser to `SafetyCopies`), then the stray file is cleared away — only once its words are in the board's own file or in `SafetyCopies`. A copy without its board becomes the board. A copy that has not arrived (`.….icloud`) is asked for and joined at a later look. (`BoardFileName` reads what a file is from its name.)
  3. A file from a newer format (`format` above 2; for Seedboard 0.1: above 1, so every board 0.2 changed), or one holding a kind this version does not know, is read and SHOWN (`canBeChanged` false, read-only) and never written over (device and iCloud writers refuse it). When two copies differ and only one is such a file, that one wins and the other goes to the safety copies.
  4. A cloud file that cannot be read, or has not arrived yet, is never written over. A DEVICE file that cannot be read no longer stops every save: its bytes are kept in `SafetyCopies` (`….unreadable`), then the new version is written.
  5. A removed board leaves a mark `<UUID>.gone` in the device `Boards` folder and the iCloud `Boards` folder, and both copies move (device and iCloud) to `Removed/<UUID>-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>.seedboard` (the next free second when that name is taken). It is not deleted at once. The mark carries WHEN the board was removed: `{"removed":"<ISO 8601 with thousandths>"}`, always later than the board's last change (`RemovalMark.time`); an old mark of 0.1 is empty and counts as "removed at the mark file's date". At each load and each look at iCloud (`SafetyRules.removal`): a version of the board changed AFTER the removal wins — the marks are cleared on the device and in iCloud and the board is back on every device; otherwise every device that finds a mark moves its own copy to its Removed folder too (`moveAway`), and the board is not shown. A save of a marked board is refused unless its change is later than the removal (then it clears the mark). While a mark or iCloud's copy of the board has not arrived yet, nothing is decided for that board. Files in either Removed folder are deleted for good when the day in their NAME is more than 30 days back (`removedDay`); a file whose day cannot be read is kept. The 30 days count from when that device moved its copy. Marks are kept a year (by creation date). `BoardStore.bringBack(id:)` puts the newest copy in Removed back as the board, stamped later than the removal, and clears the marks; the buttons for it are "Bring back" on the page of 2.7 and on the removed-link line of 2.1 (`AppModel.bringBack(removed:)`, which then lets the board join the list and go through the usual save once more so iCloud has it).
  6. Once a day, before the first write or load of the day, all device boards are copied to `SafetyCopies/<yyyy-MM-dd>/`; the newest 14 day folders are kept. Nothing is made when there are no boards.
  7. Loading: the union of ids in both folders (a `.<name>.icloud` placeholder is asked for and counted), minus the removed ones (rule 5). Both copies equal → used; iCloud's newer → it is shown, the device's version goes to the safety copies and is replaced (unless the device has moved on meanwhile: then the next save settles it); the device's newer → it is shown and written to iCloud, and iCloud's version is kept in the safety copies unless it is one this board grew from (a newer-format copy beats an older-format one, rule 3); only the device copy → shown and sent to iCloud; only the cloud copy → shown and copied to the device; a side unreadable → the other is shown and the unreadable one is left alone. A board of a newer format is shown read-only (banner of 2.3).
  8. iCloud is watched (a query on `*.seedboard` and `*.gone`, and at every return to the app); a board that is newer than what the app SHOWS is news: it replaces the board in the list, and in the open board when it is not being typed in or dragged (then Undo's steps are cleared, 3.7). News for a board that is being typed in is held and offered again (2.2); only news the app shows counts as seen, anything else is offered again at the next look (`SafetyRules.news`: ignore / take / hold). Boards that gained a removal mark on another device leave the list (2.2).
  9. A removal reaches every device through the mark of rule 5: another device that still has its copy moves it to Removed at its next load or at the next look at iCloud — unless that copy was changed after the removal.
  10. Writes that fail are said, not swallowed. The store tells the app (`onState`) two things: `notInCloud` (rule 11) and `warning` (texts in 2.5) — a device write that failed, or an iCloud write that failed. While either is the case the store looks again every few seconds (3 s, then twice as long each time, 30 s at most): it tries the failed device write again, looks at iCloud again (which writes whatever is only on the device), and stops by itself when all is well.
  11. "Not in iCloud yet": a board is in this set from the moment it is saved until iCloud's folder holds that same version AND iCloud says the file is uploaded (`ubiquitousItemIsUploaded`); also boards found at loading that are only on the device or not uploaded. Without iCloud the set is empty (Settings says "iCloud is off" instead).
  12. Known weak spots (**Note**): a copy of a newer-format board is refused; Send on such a board writes the files in PARAGON but the sent mark is not kept. With clocks that disagree between devices the "newer" change is the one with the later stamp, not always the one made last; the loser is always in `SafetyCopies`.
- Mac: Settings → Show the boards folder opens the device `Boards` folder.

### 6.1 Removed boards and earlier versions (Kept.swift, KeptFiles.swift; the page: 2.7)

- `KeptRules.keepDays` = 30. `daysLeft(since:now:)` = 30 − days gone, rounded up, never below 0 (30 on the day of removal; 12 days gone → 18). 0 is shown as "last day", 1 as "1 day left".
- `letGo(kind, kept:, now:)` — removed boards and set-aside versions: older than 30 days; daily copies: never by this rule (they keep the newest 14 day folders, rule 6).
- `newest(_:)` — one copy per board: the version changed last wins; of two copies of the same version (stamps within 1.5 ms) the one removed last. Order: most recently removed first.
- `removedRows(_:present:)` — the newest copy of every board that is NOT in the list now (a board that was brought back leaves its old copies in the Removed folders until their 30 days are over; they are not shown).
- `groups(versions:boards:removed:marked:)` — per board: versions with the same `changed` stamp are one row (kept: set-aside with a known origin, before set-aside without, before daily; then the most recent copy); a version with the stamp of the board as it stands (or of the removed copy) is left out; a board with a removal mark and no removed copy left (removed for good, or its 30 days are over) is not listed; of `.setAside(.here)` only the newest `mostOwn` = 10 are listed, the rest counted in `hidden`. Order: boards in the list by name, then removed, then lost.
- `copyTitle` — "Title (version from 18 Nov 10:00)" (the VERSION's own date, i.e. its `changed`; the year added when not this year); a taken name gets " 2", " 3". `asCopy` — new identity, that title, created and changed now, not pinned, `sent` and `sentTodos` cleared. The board itself is never replaced.
- Words: `removedLine`, `sourceLine`, `clashLine` (2.1, 2.7). Dates are "d MMM" / "d MMM HH:mm" in English, with the year when it is not this year.
- `KeptFileName` reads a kept file's name: `Removed/<id>-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>.seedboard`; `SafetyCopies/<id>-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>-<6 letters>[-here|-away].seedboard` (or `.unreadable`); `.<name>.icloud` = not arrived.
- `KeptFiles.list` — reads both Removed folders (device, iCloud), SafetyCopies (day folders `yyyy-MM-dd/<id>.seedboard` and set-aside files) and the removal marks in both Boards folders. A copy is listed only when it decodes as the board its name says.
- `tidy(safety:now:)` — deletes set-aside files (`.seedboard` and `.unreadable`) older than 30 days; called at every look at the folders (`BoardDisk.loadAll`, after `purgeRemoved`). (In 0.1 set-aside versions were never cleared away.)
- `removeForGood(id, in:)` — deletes every file of that board in the Removed folders, nothing else. The removal mark stays (so no device puts the board back); the board's safety copies stay until their own time is up but are no longer listed.
- `read(version, in:)` — reads one earlier version again (only ever a file inside SafetyCopies).
- The store's bridge: `BoardStore.kept { places in … }` runs such work on the store's iCloud line of work, off the main thread; nil with the example boards (nothing is read or written then). `BoardStore.bytes(of:)` / `board(from:)` give the board's file coding.
- **Note**: "where it came from" is the device's best knowledge within one run of the app. A version set aside before the board was shown in that run says only "set aside". In a rare order of events (iCloud's copy not readable at the first look after a restart, and the device ahead of it) the device's own older version in iCloud can be taken for another device's and raise the clash line once without need.
- **Note**: a clash where the device was restarted before it noticed (its unsent change loses against a newer one from iCloud at the next start) is kept under Earlier versions, but raises no line.
- **Note**: this device's version is set aside every time another device's change arrives, so a board edited on two devices collects many "this device's version" rows; the page lists the newest 10 per board and counts the rest. All are deleted after 30 days. The old hook `AppModel.bringBack(_:)` is no longer called and may be removed.

### 6.2 Export and import (ExportAll.swift; `AppModel.exportAllBoards`, `importBoards`)

- Export all boards (Settings, 2.5). Name: "Seedboard boards yyyy-MM-dd" + ".zip". Inside one folder of the same name: per board `<name>.seedboard` (the same bytes as the board's own file) and `<name>.md`. `<name>` = the title cleaned by PARAGON's file-name rule (`ParagonExport.noteTitle`); a second board with the same name (whatever the capitals) gets " (2)", " (3)".
- The `.md`: "# Title", a line "counts · last changed 17 Nov 10:00 · exported from Seedboard on 18 Nov", the board's outline (`Board.outlineText`, 3.10) and "To do:" with `- [ ]` lines for everything marked to do.
- The packed file is made by the system (`NSFileCoordinator`, reading the folder `.forUploading`) in the app's temporary folder, which is cleared again; nothing third-party. It is handed over with SwiftUI's `fileExporter` (Files' save sheet on iPhone and iPad, a save panel on the Mac).
- "Last export" is stored per device in the app's settings, key `lastExport` (seconds since 1970), only after the save place said yes. What is being typed is kept first, so it is in the export.
- Import boards… (`ExportRules.imported`, `looksLikeBoard`): any files may be chosen (several at once); a file counts as a board when it is JSON with `id`, `title` and `items` and this version can change it. Each becomes a new board: new identity, changed now, not pinned, not sent; a taken name gets " (imported)", " (imported 2)". The line below says "2 boards came in as new boards." and/or "1 file was not a board this Seedboard can read." The packed file itself cannot be chosen: the owner unpacks it first.

## 7. From outside, and opening where you left

### 7.1 Shortcuts actions (`Intents.swift`, App Intents, iOS 17 / macOS 14)

- `NewBoardFromTextIntent` "New board from text": Title (optional), Text (several lines). The board is made by `Outside.board(title:text:)` in SeedboardCore — the same rule as pasting: two or more non-empty lines and a readable outline → a mind map (`addMap`; one top line = the middle idea, several = the title, or "Ideas", in the middle); else one sticky note; no text → an empty board. Name: the title, else the middle idea, else the first line (40 letters at most). Gives the new board back, so a shortcut can go on with it.
- `NewBoardIntent` "New board": Start with = Blank board (default), Mind map, Flow, Pros and cons, Plan in steps. (**Note**: Timeline is not offered here.)
- `OpenBoardIntent` "Open board": Board = one of the boards, offered newest first, found by name (`Outside.boards(named:)`: the exact name, then names that begin with the words, then names that hold them). Opens it the way a link is followed, so a board removed meanwhile is said so in the app.
- All three open the app and run inside it, through the app's one model (`AppModel.shared`; `AppModel.add` gives the colour, a name of its own "… 2", saves through the store and opens). There is no second writer. They wait until the boards are read, so they work when the app was not running. Problem text: "The board could not be made."
- `SeedboardShortcuts` (`AppShortcutsProvider`), 7 phrases, each with the app's name: "New Seedboard board from text", "Start a thought in Seedboard", "New Seedboard board", "New board in Seedboard", "New <starter> in Seedboard", "Open a Seedboard board", "Open <board> in Seedboard". The board names Siri offers are refreshed when the list of names changes. Raycast runs the actions through its Shortcuts command.
- No Share-sheet action of its own; "New board from text" can be used from the Share sheet through a shortcut that takes the shared text.

### 7.2 Search on the device (`Spotlight.swift`, Core Spotlight)

- One entry per board: id = the board's id, name = the board's name, description = the first words (`Outside.searchWords`: every thing's words in the order made, one line each, joined by " · ", cut at a whole word at 300 letters, then "…"; an empty board shows its count line), never expiring.
- Told 2 s after the list of boards last changed, only for boards whose time of last change differs from what was told before (`Outside.searchPlan`; kept in `UserDefaults`, key `seedboard.searchTold`), on a background task. Boards no longer in the list are taken out of the search. With `-sampleData` nothing is told.
- Choosing a result opens the board through the same path as a `seedboard://board/<id>` link (`onContinueUserActivity`).

### 7.3 Opens where you left (`Core/Remember.swift`, `App/Sources/Remember.swift`)

- Kept in the device's own settings (`UserDefaults`, key `seedboard.remembered`, JSON): `open` (id of the open board, absent on the home page) and `views` (board id → `scale`, `x`, `y`). `x`, `y` is the board point that stood in the MIDDLE of the room (rounded to 0.1), `scale` the zoom (rounded to 0.001) — the middle, so the same spot comes back in a window of another size. Never synced, never in a board file.
- Written: when another board is opened or the board is closed, 0.6 s after the view last moved, and when the app is left or quit. A view is only written once the owner has moved or zoomed the board; before that the board keeps opening shown whole. Views of boards that are no longer on the device are dropped at each write.
- At the start (`AppModel.load`): `Reopen.board` gives the board to open — the remembered one if it is on the device, else none (home page). A link, a Shortcuts action or a search result that started the app wins over it.
- Whenever a board is opened: `Reopen.view` gives its view; `Viewport.comeBack` sets the zoom (kept within 20 %–300 %) and holds the remembered spot in the middle while the room still settles (window finding its size, side bar), until the owner slides or zooms (`Viewport.pin`, `holdPin`).
- Launch words win: with any of `-sampleData`, `-board`, `-pick`, `-pickAll`, `-send`, `-sendNow`, `-settings`, `-starters` nothing is brought back (`Reopen.sceneWords`); with `-sampleData` nothing is written down either.
