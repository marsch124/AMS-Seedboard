Seedboard 0.5 – worth trying:
1. Home page (Mac, iPad): your boards first, starters in one sliding strip.
2. iPhone: search and the round green + at the bottom of the list.
3. Zoom and Show the whole board glide; things grow in, fade out and lift. Does it feel smooth?
4. Tab in a sticky note makes the next note. Choose a few notes: Make a mind map, Tidy, By colour, Turn into.
5. Doors: press and hold empty board, Door to a board…; open it, then ‹ to go back. Try Move to a new board….
6. Timeline: Done on a bar, late bars in red, Catch up, week numbers. More, Make a timeline of it… on a mind map.
7. Send a board to PARAGON, tick a task there, come back: the purple line and the four to-do marks.
8. Send again with new tasks: Add what is new. Check What PARAGON gets on the Send page.
9. Share…: a PDF of a board, and Print….
10. Share a web page or a photo from Safari or Photos to Seedboard. Shortcuts: Add to board.
11. iPhone: hold the Picture tile (Take a photo, Scan a page); press the app icon long; three fingers to undo.
12. Mac: Help menu, Dock menu, click the board's name, Space on a picture.
