# "Who does what" board (pick 3) — builder notes

## 1. What's new

**Who does what — a new kind of board.**
Before: 6 ways to start a board (blank, mind map, flow, pros and cons, plan in steps, timeline); to show who asks whom for what, in which order, you had to place boxes and arrows by hand.
Now: a 7th starter, **Who does what** ("People in a row, messages in order"), opens a board of its own kind. The app places everything; nothing is moved pixel by pixel.

- **The names** (people, or things like a bank) stand in a row at the top, each in a box of its own colour (the first 8 names get blue, teal, orange, purple, green, pink, red, yellow), each with a thin dashed line hanging down. The lines stand 150 points apart and move further apart only where the words between them need more room.
- **A message** is a level arrow from one dashed line to another, with its words above it. The messages follow each other from top to bottom and are numbered 1, 2, 3 … by the app; the number stands in a small round mark before the words. A message takes the colour of the name it comes from, so you see at a glance who is speaking.
- **An answer** is drawn with dashes and an open arrow head.
- **A message to itself** ("Check the papers") is a small loop on the name's own line.
- **A note** is a soft band across all the lines ("Bank closed on Sundays"). **A section** is a heading on a thin line across the whole diagram ("If the loan is refused"). Neither is numbered.
- **4 tools** with their word under them: Who, Message, Note, Section.
- **Three ways to add a message**: pull sideways from one dashed line to another (a dashed line follows; a thin line shows between which two messages it will stand); or choose a name, press **Message to…** and press the other name; or choose a message and press Return for the next one under it.
- **Reorder by dragging**: a message, note or section up or down (a thin line shows where it lands), a name box sideways. The numbers follow by themselves.
- **The bar for a chosen message**: Words, Answer, Turn round, Colour, To do, Copy, Remove. **For a chosen name**: Name, Colour, Message to…, Remove. On iPhone and iPad a message also has Move up and Move down.
- **The names stay in sight**: slide down a long diagram and the name boxes stay pinned at the top.
- **Removing a name** removes its messages too, and a line says how many, with Undo ("“Bank” went with its 2 messages.").
- **Send to PARAGON** understands it: as a note the picture plus a numbered list ("1. Anna → Bank: Ask for loan", "2. Bank answers Anna: Yes"), notes and sections in place; as a project the ticked messages become tasks (with nothing ticked, all of them do).
- The made-up sample boards got a 10th board, "Loan steps" (4 names, 9 messages, 1 note, 1 section, 1 message to itself).

**Fixed: the Timeline starter's small picture on the home page was blank.**
Before: the starter card "Timeline" showed an empty picture. Why: the starter's example was made for 1 January 2001, the picture always includes today, so 25 years had to fit into 240 points and the bars became too small to see.
Now: the Timeline starter's example is made for today; its 3 bars and its milestone show in the card. The new "Who does what" card shows its three names and three messages.

**Stored format: still 2.** Three new optional fields on a thing (`from`, `to`, `answer`) and four new kinds (`who`, `message`, `band`, `section`); the board's `kind` is `"sequence"`.

## 2. How it works (guide text)

**Who does what**

A "Who does what" board shows who asks whom for what, and in which order: the names stand in a row at the top, and the messages run from top to bottom, numbered by the app. Start one with **New board ▸ Who does what**; it opens with a small example (Anna, Bank, Builder).

- **Add a name** — press **Who** at the bottom (or the key W). The new name appears at the right end of the row and you type it straight away. Press **Tab** while typing and the next name begins beside it; Return ends the typing.
- **Add a message** — put the pointer or your finger on the dashed line under a name and pull sideways to another dashed line. A dashed line follows, the name you are heading for gets a dashed ring, and a thin line shows between which two messages the new one will stand: the height where you let go decides its place. Then type its words.
- **Or without pulling** — choose a name, press **Message to…** and press the name it goes to. Or press **Message** (M), then the name it comes from, then the name it goes to. Such a message goes under the last one.
- **The next message** — choose a message and press **Return** (or press Tab while you type its words): the next message appears right under it, going the other way. After a question it is an answer; after an answer it is a new question.
- **An answer** — choose a message and press **Answer**: it is drawn with dashes. Press Answer again to make it a plain message. A message you pull back the other way right under a question is made an answer for you.
- **A message to itself** — choose the name, press **Message to…** and press the same name again. Or pull out from its dashed line and back onto it in a small loop.
- **Turn round** — the chosen message now comes from the name it went to.
- **Another order** — drag a message, note or section up or down; a thin line shows where it will land, and the numbers follow. On the Mac you can also hold Option and press ↑ or ↓. On iPhone and iPad rest your finger on the message for a moment before you drag (a quick slide moves the view), or press **Move up** / **Move down**.
- **Another place in the row** — drag a name box sideways; a thin upright line shows where it will stand. Its messages come along. On the Mac: Option with ← or →.
- **A note** — press **Note** (N): a soft band across the lines, under the chosen message or at the end. **A section** — press **Section** (S): a heading on a line across the whole diagram, for a new part ("If the loan is refused").
- **Change the words** — press a name or a message twice, or choose it and just type. On the iPhone choose it and press **Name** or **Words**. Return ends the typing, Escape cancels it.
- **Colour** — a name has its own colour; a message takes the colour of the name it comes from. Choose a message and press **Colour** to give it a colour of its own; the first dot ("No colour") gives it back the colour of its name.
- **To do** — marks the chosen message with the purple tick; it goes to PARAGON as a task.
- **Copy, Remove** — Copy puts a copy right under the message. Remove (or the Delete key) takes it away. Removing a name removes every message from it and to it as well; a line at the bottom says how many went, with Undo.
- **Zoom and slide** — as on every board: the zoom buttons, a pinch, ⌘+ and ⌘−; the frame button shows the whole diagram (⌘0). Drag an empty place to slide, or scroll with two fingers on the Mac. The names stay at the top while you slide down.
- **Keys** — ↑ and ↓ choose the message above or below, ← and → the name before or after; Space or Return (on a name, a note, a section) starts typing.

**Send to PARAGON**

- **As a note** — the diagram's picture, the names, then the messages as a numbered list: "1. Anna → Bank: Ask for loan", "2. Bank answers Anna: Yes", "3. Bank, by itself: Check the papers". A note stands as a quoted line and a section as a heading, each in its place. Ticked messages are listed again under Tasks.
- **As a project** — the messages in their order under Tasks. The ones you ticked To do become tasks and the others stay plain lines; with nothing ticked, all of them become tasks. Sections are headings; notes go under the project's Notes.
- **Only the to-do boxes** — the ticked messages go to the Inbox as tasks, with their own words.

## 3. Spec

**Stored (format 2)**
- Board: `kind = "sequence"` (`Board.sequenceKind`; `Board.isSequence`). New: `Board.placesItself` = timeline or sequence.
- New item kinds (`ItemKind`): `who` (word "Who"), `message` ("Message"), `band` ("Note"), `section` ("Section"). `isStep` = message, band, section; `isSequence` = those and who.
- New optional item fields, each on its own coding-key line, tolerant decoding, written only when set: `from` (UUID), `to` (UUID) — the two names of a message, equal for a message to itself; `answer` (Bool, written only when true; `Item.isAnswer`).
- Reused fields: `text` (name or words), `colour`, `todo` (messages only), `order` (a name's place in the row; a step's place from the top — names and steps are numbered 1…n separately after every change), `w` and `h` (the room the words take, measured by the app in `Lettering.fit`; nothing else of `x y w h` is used).
- Golden list for format 2 in `StoredFieldsTests`: item list now also holds `answer`, `from`, `to`.
- An older 0.1 app shows such a board read-only (unknown kinds; its own rule).

**Rules (Core/Sources/SeedboardCore/Sequence.swift)**
- `whos`, `steps`: by `order`, ties in the order made. `messageNumbers`: 1, 2, 3 … over messages only.
- `addWho(name, at:, colour:)`: at the right end without a place; colour = the first of blue, teal, orange, purple, green, pink, red, yellow that no name wears (then round again).
- `addMessage(text, from:, to:, at:, answer:)`: nil unless both are names on the board; a place past either end is clamped. `addBand`, `addSection` likewise.
- `wouldAnswer(from:to:at:)`: true when the step right above is a message the other way between the same two names and not an answer itself. Used for pulled and "Message to…" messages.
- `addMessageAfter(id)` (Return): right under it, from/to swapped, answer = the chosen one was not an answer (never for a message to itself).
- `moveStep(id, to:)`, `moveWho(id, to:)`: place counted without the moved thing; true when the place changed.
- `turnMessageRound(id)`: swaps from and to.
- `removeFromSequence(ids)`: a name takes every message from or to it; returns how many messages went along that were not chosen themselves. `messageCount(of:)`.
- `duplicateInSequence(ids)`: a step's copy right under it; a name's copy right beside it (no messages).
- `sequenceCountLine`: "4 people · 9 messages · 1 note · 1 section" ("Empty" for none; singular "1 person", "1 message").
- `stepLine(id)`: "2. Bank answers Anna: Yes"; `sequenceText` (Copy the board as text): "Who: Anna, Bank, Builder", then the numbered lines, "   Note: …", "<section>:".
- Search on the home page finds names and words (they are ordinary item texts).

**Layout (`SequenceMetrics`, `SequenceLayout`; board points, the diagram's box starts at 0,0)**
- Margin left and right 28; above the names 20. Name box: name + 14 each side, at least 92 wide; height 34 or the tallest name + 16; corner 9.
- Dashed lines at least 150 apart; at least (half of both boxes + 24). Wider where needed: a message between neighbours needs number 18 + 5 + words + 14 each side; a message across several gaps shares what is missing evenly between those gaps; a message to itself needs 30 (loop) + 8 + number + words + 14 to the right (on the last name the diagram grows to the right).
- First step 12 under the names. A message: 9 above its words, words (17 a line, wrapping at 240), 4, the arrow, 9 under it — 39 for one line of words. A message to itself: loop 30 wide, 20 tall. A note: band with 6 above and under its words, 6 room above and under the band; as wide as the diagram inside the margins; words wrap at 360. A section: 14 above, 8 under; its line runs across the whole width; words wrap at 420.
- The dashed lines end 40 under the last step, never shorter than 96; 16 under them.
- `slot(atY:moving:)`: the new place = how many other steps have their middle above the point. `landingY(slot:moving:)`: the edge between the two steps. `whoSlot(atX:moving:)`, `whoLandingX`. `who(nearX:reach:)`. `pinShift(top:inset:)`: how far the names move down to stay in sight.
- `Board.bounds` of a sequence = that box (nil when empty), so "whole board", the first view, the small picture and the PARAGON picture all use it.

**Look (App/Sources/SequenceDraw.swift, one painter for the open board, the home page picture and the PARAGON picture)**
- Name: 15 semibold, soft fill of its colour, outline 1.7 in its colour, faint shadow by day. Dashed line: 1.3, dashes 5/5, the name's colour at 65 %.
- Message: line 1.8 in its own colour or the colour of the name it comes from; filled head 9.5 × 9.2; a 5-point dot where it starts. Answer: dashes 6/4, open head. Number: round mark 18 across, bold 10.5, white by day and dark by night. Words 14, on a patch of the board's own colour so a dashed line never runs through the letters. To do: PARAGON's purple ring with a tick, 11 right of the words.
- Note: soft fill (yellow when it has no colour), outline at 50 %, words 13.5 centred. Section: line 1.2, words 13.5 semibold.
- Chosen: green ring 2; a message also gets a soft green glow along its line. "Message to…" start and the name a pull heads for: dashed green ring. Dragged thing: drawn at 80 % where the finger is, its place at 30 %.
- Pinned names: 8 points under the top edge (52 while a line of words lies over the top), on a strip of the board's colour with a hairline under it.
- New app part `Part.sequence`: olive green #5C7A12 by day, #B5D95A by night (Who tool, Answer, Colour, Move up/down).

**Screen (SequenceView.swift, SequenceSession.swift, SequenceBars.swift)**
- `BoardView` shows `SequenceView` when `board.isSequence`; tools `SequenceToolPill`; bar `SequenceSelectionBar` at the bottom; zoom buttons, Undo, top bar, Send as on every board.
- What a drag does: sideways from a dashed line (within 11 screen points of it) = pull a message; sideways on a name box = move it in the row; up or down on a step = move it in the order (iPhone/iPad: after resting 0.25 s, with a light tap of the motor); anything else slides the view. A pull lands on the nearest dashed line within 44 screen points (at most 0.4 of 150); back on its own line only after having been 30 screen points away = a message to itself; let go on nothing = nothing.
- Press: chooses; twice = type; Shift-press (Mac) or Choose more (iPhone/iPad) adds to what is chosen. A press on a dashed line chooses its name. While a message is being made only names (or their lines) count; a press on empty board cancels.
- Keys (nothing being typed): W, M, N, S tools; Return = next message (on a message) or type (others); Tab on a name = next name; ↑ ↓ ← →; Option+arrows move; Space = type; Delete; Escape; ⌘A; any letter starts typing in the chosen thing. While typing: Return = done, Escape = cancel, Tab = next name / next message. iPhone/iPad keyboard bar: "Next name" or "Next message", Done.
- Empty board: "Nobody here yet" with one line of help.
- Every change is one Undo step (`change`); typing as on every board.
- `BoardSession` hooks (one line each): `paste` (does nothing on a sequence), `deleteSelection`, `duplicateSelection`, `toggleTodo` (messages only), `reveal`.

**Send (Sequence.swift, `ParagonExport`)**
- `messageLine`: "Anna → Bank: Ask for loan"; answer "Bank answers Anna: Yes"; to itself "Bank, by itself: Check the papers"; no words → ends after the names; a name without words is "(no name)".
- Note (`sequenceNote`, Resources): front matter, `# title`, picture line, link back, `## Who` (names joined with " · "), `## Who does what` (numbered list; a note as `> …`, a section as `### …`, blank lines round both), `## Tasks` (ticked messages as `- [ ] Anna → Builder: Order the build`).
- Project (`sequenceProject`, Projects): `## Outcome` (the title), `## Tasks` (every message in order: `- [ ] …` for a task, `- …` for a plain line; sections as `###`), `## Notes` ("Who: …", the notes as bullets), `## Log`.
- Ticks: with at least one ticked message only ticked ones are tasks; with none all are (`sequenceTicksDecide`, `sequenceTaskLines`). Send page line (`sequenceProjectLine`): "2 ticked messages become tasks, 7 stay as plain lines." / "Nothing is ticked: all 9 messages become tasks."
- Only the to-do boxes: the generic rule (ticked items with words, not yet sent) — only messages can be ticked.
- The picture: `BoardPainter.draw` hands a sequence to `SequencePainter.whole`.

## 4. For the lead

- **Launch arguments for pictures**: `-sampleData -board Loan` opens the made-up "Loan steps" board (`App/Sources/SequenceSample.swift`, one added line in `Sample.boards`). `-pick <word>` (the existing one) chooses a name or a message by its words: `-pick "Order the build"` (a ticked message → the message bar), `-pick Bank` (a name → the name bar), `-pick Sundays` (the note), `-pick refused` (the section). `-type` with `-pick` starts typing in it. `-send -sendAs project` shows the Send page with the sequence words. `-night` as always. The starter card: the home page (`home.starter.sequence`).
- **Screen test**: `UITests/SequenceUITests.swift`, one test, written, not run.
- **Not run by me**: no simulator, no app launch, no screen test. I drew the painter to picture files with a throwaway command-line program (day, night, pinned names) to check the look; the gestures, the typing fields and the bars were only compiled.
- **ICON-WANTED**: a section — a short heading stroke sitting on a long thin level line (the Section tool shows `.new(.lanes)` until then).
- **ICON-WANTED**: a note across the lines — two short upright dashed lines with a soft level band across them (the Note tool shows the sticky-note mark until then).
- `.new(.person)` is the Who tool, `.new(.answer)` the Answer button. `.new(.sequence)` is not used on a screen yet (the starter card shows the real small drawing, as for the timeline); it is free for the Mac's menus.
- **Shared files touched** (all small hunks): `Board.swift` (kinds, three fields), `BoardEdit.swift` (`bounds`, `countLine`), `Outline.swift`, `Paragon.swift` (2 lines), `ProjectPlan.swift` (1 line), `Starters.swift`, `Theme.swift` (`Part.sequence`), `CanvasDraw.swift` (`Lettering.fit`, `BoardPainter.draw`, `drawItem`), `BoardView.swift` (8 small places; `isTimeline` became `placesItself` in three of them), `BoardSession.swift` (5 lines), `SendSheet.swift` (2 lines), `BoardThumb.swift` (the timeline starter's date), `Sample.swift` (1 line), `SafetyTests.swift` (golden list), `StartersTests.swift`.
- **Merge note**: other builders adding `ItemKind` cases will clash with my line `case who, message, band, section` and with the `switch` lines beside it (`word`, `defaultSize`, `Lettering.fit`, `drawItem`, `itemChips`); keep both sides.
- **Left open / for another builder**:
  - The Mac's Board menu: "Line Up", "Even Gaps", "Same Size" and "Paste a List as a Mind Map" are not switched off on a sequence board. Paste does nothing; the three tidy commands would only write places nothing reads (one empty Undo step). They should be disabled where `board.placesItself` — the same holds for a timeline.
  - The Mac menu builder may want "Move the Message Up / Down" (I used Option with the arrow keys, because ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ belong to the idea commands).
  - A note always runs across all the lines; a note over just one or two names is not built.
  - Words in a message's typing field are one line while typing, also when they wrap to two lines once done.
  - "Only the to-do boxes" sends a message's bare words ("Order the build"), without the two names.
- **Only a real device can prove**: pulling a message with a finger (does a sideways pull from a dashed line feel right, is 11 points enough to catch the line on an iPhone); the 0.25 s rest before a message lifts; pinch while a drag is on; the keyboard bar buttons on iPhone; that Tab reaches the field on an iPad with a keyboard; the pinned names while scrolling fast on 120 Hz; the first real Send of a sequence into the real notes (no real Send was run).
